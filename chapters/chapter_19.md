# Chapter 19

**Mount Myōken, Hyōgo \| September 15, 2120 \| 10:30**

After Kael regained his hearing and was finally able to move without
much difficulty, he asked Iris to perform a complete scan of both him
and Cherry. He wanted to know exactly what had changed after everything
that had happened beneath the Giant Tree.

### Kael Ardyn -- IRIS Status Report

**Age:** 21

**Time Post-Awakening:** 37 Days

**Location:** Mount Myōken, Hyōgo

**Date:** September 15, 2120 \| **Time:** 10:30

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Core Attribute**             **Value**
  ------------------------------ ----------------------------------------
  Strength                       **8.54** (from 2.78)

  Speed                          **8.04** (from 2.48)

  Life Force                     **8.93** (from 2.97)

  Stamina                        **8.92** (from 2.96)

  Constitution                   **7.32** (from 2.42)

  Reaction                       **0.20 s** (from 0.22 s)
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

Kael stared at the report, unable to say a word.

Nearly every attribute had increased to roughly three times its previous
value.

He slowly clenched his right hand.

His fingers closed effortlessly.

The muscles beneath his skin felt tighter, denser.

Without thinking, he squeezed harder.

*Crack.*

The stone beneath his palm split into several pieces.

Kael immediately loosened his grip and looked at the broken rock.

\"\...I barely used any strength.\"

\"Your physical output has increased beyond what your nervous system is
accustomed to,\" Iris explained. \"Based on your current condition, your
body is healthy. I detected no injuries or abnormalities.\"

\"So\...there\'s nothing wrong with me?\"

\"I found nothing that suggests your life is in danger.\"

Kael nodded.

\"Alright.\"

\"I\'ll scan Cherry now.\"

A tiny micro-needle extended from the wrist device and lightly pricked
Cherry.

The kitten merely flicked one ear before sitting down as if nothing had
happened.

Moments later another report appeared.

### Cherry -- IRIS Status Report

**Age:** 2 Months

**Time Post-Awakening:** 37 Days

**Location:** Mount Myōken, Hyōgo

**Date:** September 15, 2120 \| **Time:** 10:30

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Core Attribute**               **Value**
  -------------------------------- --------------------------------------
  Strength                         **7.60** (from 3.80)

  Speed                            **15.43** (from 9.86)

  Life Force                       **8.20** (from 4.10)

  Stamina                          **8.00** (from 4.00)

  Constitution                     **8.40** (from 4.20)
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

Kael blinked.

\"Well\...I understand why I became stronger.\"

\"But how did this little fellow nearly double her strength?\"

\"Don\'t tell me the Giant Tree fed Cherry a fruit too.\"

\"That is possible,\" Iris replied.

\"The Giant Tree may have given Cherry a similar fruit.\"

Kael looked at Cherry before grinning.

\"At least I\'m finally stronger than you.\"

Cherry tilted her head.

\"I don\'t have to feel embarrassed that my pet is stronger than me
anymore.\"

The tiny kitten immediately puffed up.

\"Hsssss!\"

She glared at him with obvious displeasure.

Kael burst into laughter.

He picked her up and scratched beneath her chin.

\"Relax.\"

\"You\'re not my pet.\"

\"You\'re my partner.\"

Cherry\'s ears relaxed.

She let out a proud little purr before rubbing her head against his
chest.

Suddenly\...

She vanished.

Kael\'s eyes widened.

A heartbeat later she was sitting nearly twenty meters away on the
branch of a massive mutated cedar.

Before he could even react---

She disappeared again.

Another blur.

She landed on his shoulder without making a sound.

\"\...\"

Kael sighed.

\"I take it back.\"

\"You\'re still terrifying.\"

Cherry proudly purred.

\"I believe Cherry remains considerably more dangerous in actual
combat,\" Iris added.

\"Her speed exceeds yours by a significant margin.\"

Kael chuckled.

\"Fine.\"

\"So I\'m only stronger physically.\"

\"I can live with that.\"

He gently placed Cherry on the ground.

\"Kael.\"

Iris spoke again.

\"Your increased physical capabilities require immediate adaptation.\"

\"You currently possess enough strength to unintentionally injure
yourself or lose efficiency during combat.\"

She projected several movement diagrams.

\"I recommend beginning with controlled movement.\"

\"Walking.\"

\"Running.\"

\"Jumping.\"

\"Climbing.\"

\"Only after your movements become natural should we begin hunting
mutated animals.\"

Kael immediately frowned.

\"Wait.\"

\"You actually want me to hunt them?\"

\"Why?\"

\"Wouldn\'t it be safer to avoid every fight until we reach Kyoto?\"

\"There are four reasons.\"

Iris answered immediately.

\"First.\"

\"You require practical combat experience.\"

\"No amount of movement training can replace real combat.\"

\"Second.\"

\"Mutated creatures provide food and it allow your body to become
stronger.\"

\"Third.\"

\"Your destination lies nearly one hundred kilometers away.\"

\"There is an extremely high probability that hostile encounters are
unavoidable.\"

\"And fourth\...\"

She became quieter.

\"You told me what the voice said.\"

Kael\'s smile disappeared.

\"\'You possess the potential to lead the people of this planet.\'\"

\"You continue to question those words.\"

\"That is understandable.\"

\"But leadership during humanity\'s current situation requires more than
compassion.\"

\"It requires the ability to protect others.\"

She paused briefly.

\"Historical data consistently shows that leadership without strength
collapses during crises.\"

\"Strength without compassion collapses as well.\"

\"Both are necessary.\"

Kael lowered his head.

\"\...Do I really have what it takes?\"

\"The world\'s governments\...\"

\"The Sovereign Defense Directorates\...\"

\"They possess weapons capable of destroying cities.\"

\"They should be able to stop something like this.\"

Iris answered without hesitation.

\"Kael.\"

\"We attacked the Giant Tree with everything we had.\"

\"Pulse weapons.\"

\"Combat machines.\"

\"Explosives.\"

\"Even Cherry, who is strong enough to kill a normal human in a single
attack, couldn't damage even a single leaf .\"

She continued.

\"The voice you encountered probably halted time itself for
approximately two hours.\"

\"If a being capable of such a feat tells you that an even greater
threat approaches Earth from beyond the stars\...\"

\"\...then humanity\'s existing Sovereign forces is almost certainly
insufficient.\"

Silence settled over the forest.

Only the distant rustling of leaves broke the stillness.

After a while Iris spoke again.

\"I do not know whether you will become humanity\'s leader.\"

\"No available evidence can confirm that.\"

\"But I do know this.\"

\"If humanity is to survive\...\"

\"It will need people willing to grow stronger than they are today.\"

Kael closed his eyes.

Those words lingered in his mind.

He wasn\'t convinced.

Not completely.

He still couldn\'t imagine himself leading anyone.

Leading the entire world?

That felt impossible.

Cherry climbed onto his lap.

She rubbed against his chest before repeatedly licking his cheek.

Kael laughed despite himself.

\"Alright.\"

\"I\'m fine.\"

\"You can stop worrying.\"

Cherry purred louder.

Kael slowly stood.

As he did, he accidentally pushed against a nearby tree for balance.

*Crack.*

The thick trunk split with a loud snap.

His eyes widened.

\"\...Okay.\"

\"I definitely need training.\"

For the first time Kael smiled with genuine determination.

\"I don\'t know whether I can become the person that voice spoke
about.\"

\"But I can become stronger than I was yesterday.\"

He looked toward the endless forest stretching before him.

\"Tell me, Iris.\"

\"Where do we begin?\"

Kael spent the next several hours doing nothing more than walking.

It sounded simple.

It wasn\'t.

Every step carried him farther than he intended.

Every jump launched him nearly twice as high as expected.

When he tried climbing a tree, his fingers dug into the bark so deeply
that chunks of wood tore free.

Several times he nearly lost his balance after pushing off the ground
with too much force.

Cherry watched the entire performance from a nearby branch.

Every time Kael stumbled, she tilted her head before letting out what
sounded suspiciously like an amused little chirp.

\"I believe she is laughing at you,\" Iris observed.

\"I noticed.\"

By early afternoon, Kael\'s movements had become noticeably smoother.

He no longer crushed every branch he grabbed or overshot every leap.

Then Iris spoke.

\"Movement control has improved by approximately thirty-seven percent.\"

\"There is only one way to continue improving.\"

Kael already knew the answer.

\"\...Combat.\"

\"Correct,\" Iris replied. \"I have detected a mutated animal
approximately eight hundred and fifty meters from our current location.
We will use it for your training.\"

Kael nodded and quietly made his way toward the location Iris had
marked.

Several minutes later, he crouched behind the trunk of a massive cedar
tree.

A deep, guttural grunt echoed through the forest.

*Huff\... Huff\...*

Heavy stamping shook the ground.

The wet sound of flesh being torn apart carried through the air.

Kael carefully peeked around the tree.

A gigantic black boar was devouring the carcass of a white Japanese
macaque.

The beast measured nearly three meters in length and weighed close to
four hundred kilograms. Its body resembled a living boulder covered in
coarse black fur. A massive muscular hump rose above its shoulders,
while its thick neck and heavily reinforced snout were built for digging
and devastating charges. Two ivory tusks, each nearly thirty-five
centimeters long, curved upward like polished blades.

\"I believe it is a mutated Japanese wild boar,\" Iris said quietly.

\"Based on the vibrations produced by its footsteps, I estimate its
weight to be between three hundred and fifty and four hundred
kilograms.\"

She continued analyzing the creature.

\"Judging from its muscle density and skeletal structure, it should
possess approximately five times the physical strength of an average
adult human.\"

A brief pause followed.

\"With your current physical capabilities, defeating it should not
present a significant challenge.\"

\"Alright.\"

\"However,\" Iris continued, \"I will not assist you during this
battle.\"

Kael raised an eyebrow.

\"No battle predictions.\"

\"No movement guidance.\"

\"No warnings.\"

\"While I am capable of improving your combat efficiency through
real-time assistance, doing so would prevent your own instincts from
developing.\"

\"You must learn to observe.\"

\"You must learn to react.\"

\"You must learn to fight.\"

\"On your own.\"

A faint smile appeared on Kael\'s face.

\"If you trust me that much\...\"

\"\...then I can\'t afford to disappoint you.\"

Taking a slow breath, Kael stepped out from behind the tree.

The mutated boar immediately froze.

Its blood-red eyes locked onto the unexpected intruder.

It let out an earth-shaking roar before abandoning the macaque carcass
and charging directly toward him.

The ground trembled beneath its enormous weight.

Kael lowered his spear.

Instead of attacking, he bent his knees slightly.

*Not yet.*

The distance rapidly closed.

Five meters.

Four.

Three.

When the boar came within two meters---

Kael pushed off the ground.

*Boom!*

The earth cracked beneath his feet.

His body shot sideways with explosive force, carrying him more than
three meters through the air before landing lightly on the forest floor.

His eyes widened for an instant.

*That jump was much stronger than I intended.*

The boar skidded to a halt before turning around with an angry snort.

It charged again.

Kael dodged once more.

Again.

And again.

Each jump became smoother than the last.

His landings grew more controlled.

His balance steadier.

Meanwhile, the boar became increasingly frustrated.

It roared loudly, tearing up the earth with its tusks before launching
another reckless charge.

After several failed attempts, instinct finally overcame aggression.

Unable to catch its prey, the beast sensed that its opponent was too
dangerous.

Survival took priority.

With one final snort, it turned around and began fleeing into the
forest.

Kael watched calmly.

\"I think\...it\'s time.\"

He planted one foot firmly on the ground.

In a single burst of speed, he appeared directly in front of the
escaping boar.

The beast barely had time to react.

Kael drew back his fist.

Then punched with his full strength.

*BOOM!*

A thunderous impact echoed across the forest.

The boar\'s massive body stopped instantly before being thrown sideways
several meters.

It crashed through two small trees before slamming heavily onto the
ground.

The creature struggled desperately to stand.

Blood poured from its mouth.

Kael didn\'t hesitate.

He closed the distance in an instant.

His spear plunged downward.

*Thud.*Once.

*Thud.*Twice.

*Thud.*Three times.

The boar\'s body twitched briefly before falling completely still.

Silence returned to the forest.

Kael let out a slow breath.

\"That\... was easier than I expected.\"

\"Your physical strength has increased far beyond its previous level,\"
Iris replied.

\"This creature simply wasn\'t powerful enough to challenge your current
abilities.\"

She added,

\"It will also provide an excellent meal. Your body expended
considerable energy during the training session. Consuming mutated meat
should help replenish it.\"

Cherry\'s ears perked up.

\"Meow!\"

She immediately began circling the boar excitedly.

Kael laughed.

\"If you want lunch\...then you\'ll have to help.\"

Cherry stopped and looked up at him attentively.

\"I need dry branches.\"

\"Lots of them.\"

\"We\'re starting a fire.\"

Before Kael had even finished speaking---

Cherry disappeared.

Only a white blur remained.

\"\...She\'s getting faster.\"

Meanwhile, Kael began skinning the mutated boar.

Thanks to his increased strength, removing the thick hide proved far
easier than expected.

He carefully drained the blood before separating several large cuts of
meat.

By the time he finished\...

Cherry had returned.

Not with a few branches.

But with what looked like an entire pile of firewood.

Kael stared at the miniature mountain of dry branches.

\"\...I may have underestimated your enthusiasm.\"

Cherry proudly lifted her head.

After lighting the fire, Kael slowly roasted several thick slabs of boar
meat.

The rich aroma soon filled the surrounding forest.

Fat dripped onto the flames with a satisfying sizzle.

Compared to the bland nutrient pods and energy drinks he had eaten his
entire life\...

The smell alone felt heavenly.

\"Kael,\" Iris suddenly said.

\"Why don\'t you add a few spices?\"

\"Spices?\"

Kael blinked.

\"What are spices?\"

Several holographic images appeared before him.

Leaves. Seeds. Roots. Small flowering plants.

Each was accompanied by identification data.

\"They are natural ingredients primarily used to improve flavor, aroma,
and sometimes preserve food.\"

\"Many are growing nearby.\"

Kael looked around in surprise.

\"So many?\"

\"I always wanted to ask you something, Iris.\"

\"How do you know all of this?\"

\"And according to Grandpa Tanaka, weren\'t these plants almost
extinct?\"

\"They were not extinct,\" Iris replied.

\"They were hidden.\"
