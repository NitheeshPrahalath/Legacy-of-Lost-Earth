# **Chapter 9**

**Automated Retail Store, Osaka Ruins \| September 11, 2120, 09:45**

Everyone's eyes were fixed on Kael now.

Kael felt his throat tighten. The weight of their suspicion, their
confusion, their fear---it all pressed down on him at once.\
*Iris... what do I do now?* he asked mentally, keeping his expression
calm.

Iris's voice came steady and composed.\
"Relax, Kael. First, tell them the truth---that you heard about
old-world food practices from your parents when they researched AI
development and historical lifestyles. Second, if they demand proof of
your strength, then show them. Fight a mutated animal. Cook its meat.
Eat it. Demonstrate that it is safe.\
But remember: choosing to eat it or not will be *their* decision. You do
not need to prove anything beyond what you've already survived."

Kael let out a slow breath. He stayed silent for a few seconds,
organizing his thoughts, then finally looked at the group.

"I can show you my improved strength," Kael said, voice steady. "But
whether any of you eat mutated meat or not---that's entirely your
choice. I won't force or convince anyone."

A thick silence followed.\
Even Cherry, sensing the tension, pressed herself against his chest and
let out a soft *prrrrt*.\
The gentle sound broke the heaviness in the air.

Tanaka exhaled, his posture relaxing a little.\
"Let's leave the details for later," he said. "Come inside first."

He stepped back and welcomed Kael into the retail store.

Once inside, he formally introduced himself.\
"I am Aoi Tanaka," he began. "I served in the **Sovereign Defense
Directorate** long ago, but I'm retired now. Managed an old family
library before all this happened."

He pointed to the stern-looking man with the electric baton.\
"And this is my son,Aoi Nakamura---currently serving as a
**Commander-Regent** in the **Izumo Coastal Base, shimane.**"

Nakamura gave a short nod, his expression unreadable.\
"Hello, Kael. It's good to meet you," he said in a deep, disciplined
tone.

Tanaka then gestured toward the grumpy but protective woman and the
young girl beside her.\
"This is my neighbour, Yamamoto Maya, and her daughter, Emily."

Maya shifted awkwardly, her grip tightening on Emily's hand.\
"Hello," she said, her voice softer than before. "I know I came off
harsh earlier... but I was just being cautious. I hope you understand."

She nudged her daughter gently.\
Emily stepped forward, released her mother's hand, and bowed politely.

"Hello," she said shyly. "It's nice to meet you. I'm Yamamoto Emily .
I'm twelve years old, a sixth grader at Gojō Elementary School."

Cherry wiggled in Kael's arms, staring at the girl with wide, curious
eyes. Emily involuntarily smiled back.

Tanaka guided everyone toward the back of the shop, where the floor was
less broken and a few intact display shelves formed something like a
small room. It was the safest, cleanest spot available.

As they sat down, Cherry immediately leapt from Kael's arms and began
exploring---sniffing corners, hopping onto overturned boxes, and darting
back and forth with playful bursts of energy.

Everyone's eyes followed her movements.

Even though Kael had insisted she was an ordinary cat, suspicion still
lingered in their expressions.\
But watching her tumble around like a curious kitten softened that
unease.

Emily watched Cherry with round, sparkling eyes.\
When Cherry noticed the girl\'s attention, she chirped a gentle *mrrrnt*
and padded over, tail raised.

Maya stiffened instantly.

But Emily reached out, slowly brushing her fingers through Cherry's
pristine white fur.\
Cherry responded with a soft, rumbling purr and pushed her head against
Emily's hand.

Soon enough, the two were playing---Emily giggling softly, Cherry
batting lightly at her sleeves.

Seeing her daughter unharmed, Maya's stiff shoulders gradually loosened.
She found herself... simply watching them. Almost mesmerized.

Meanwhile, Tanaka spoke quietly with Nakamura in a corner, the father's
voice low and controlled, the son's deeper and tense.

Hirota moved closer to Kael.

He leaned toward him with genuine curiosity.\
"Kael... earlier you said eating mutated animals increases strength.
Does that mean you're... really stronger than before?"

The moment he asked, the air in the room shifted.

Everyone's ears perked.\
Maya, Nakamura, even Tanaka paused their conversation.\
All eyes turned toward Kael and Hirota.

Only Cherry and Emily were too absorbed in their small game to notice.

Kael answered calmly, "Yes. After heating and cooking the meat properly,
eating it really does strengthen the body. I've tested it many times."

He stood up.

Then he walked to a half-broken cement wall near the corner---about five
inches thick---and took a stance. He inhaled slowly, tightening his
fist.

Everyone instinctively held their breath.

Even Cherry and Emily paused to look.

Kael exhaled---and punched.

**BANG.**

The impact cracked through the room.\
Dust jolted loose from the wall, and the slab visibly *shuddered*, a
small indentation appearing where his fist landed.

A moment of stunned silence filled the place.

Hirota's jaw was half open.\
Maya's eyes widened, her hand instinctively pulling Emily closer.\
Even Nakamura, trained and disciplined, blinked sharply, unable to mask
his surprise.\
Emily whispered, "Woooah..."

Tanaka stepped forward, face composed but eyes gleaming with
astonishment.

"Kael... the strength behind that punch far exceeds what your build
suggests."\
His voice was low, analyzing.\
"That force is approaching what professional fighters used to train
years for. It appears the mutated meat truly enhanced you."

Kael scratched the back of his neck, a little embarrassed by the praise
but trying to remain steady.

"Tanaka, I believe that should be enough to show that my strength has
increased, right?"

Tanaka nodded slowly.\
"Yes. I no longer doubt that eating mutated meat strengthened you.
But..."\
He exhaled deeply.\
"Whether it will improve *our* strength---or harm us---that remains
uncertain."

Before Kael could speak, Tanaka continued.

"I know we cannot prove it. But after seeing your punch... I've made my
decision. I will gamble on it not harming me."

Kael stiffened.\
He hadn't expected the old man to decide so quickly.

"Father, what are you saying?!" Nakamura burst out, his voice sharper
than before.\
"Why risk everything on untested meat?! We don't know how your body will
react!"

Tanaka looked at his son with calm, unwavering eyes.

"Nakamura... we both know my current strength is insufficient. Even with
your help, even with a pulse gun... I cannot cross the Yodo River in
this condition."\
He touched the pulse gun's worn grip.\
"These weapons won't last. Energy cells are nearly depleted."\
Then he looked around the group.\
"We need a different kind of strength. A sustainable one."

Silence swallowed the room.

Everyone felt the weight of his words.

Finally, Tanaka turned to Kael once more.\
"Kael... I'm sorry to trouble you. But I hope you can help us grow
stronger too."

Kael shook his head immediately.\
"It's not trouble, Tanaka. If eating mutated meat can help us survive...
then let's do it. We'll hunt one together and eat its meat."

Nakamura spoke suddenly.\
"We actually have the corpse of a mutated raccoon. We killed it this
morning while securing the area. Will that suffice?"

Kael mentally checked with Iris.

"Kael, if it's been dead for less than four hours, the energy content
should still be stable. It's usable."

Kael nodded to the group.\
"If it was killed within the last four hours, it should be fine."

Nakamura and Hirota went out to retrieve it while Kael prepared the
area.

Before he began, he turned to Maya.\
"Maya-san, it's better if Emily waits a little farther away. Skinning
the raccoon might be... unpleasant."

Maya immediately nodded and ushered her daughter behind a shelf.

Kael unsheathed his small blade and began skinning the raccoon.\
Iris guided him mentally on the proper method---clean cuts, avoiding the
organs, preserving the edible sections.

Tanaka, Nakamura, and Hirota watched closely---first with caution, then
with growing fascination.

His precision.

His confidence.

His uncanny knowledge of cooking techniques almost no one in this era
used.

Then Kael used metal fragments and debris to strike sparks, igniting the
collected leaves and branches.\
Soon, a modest flame crackled to life.

He cooked the meat for twenty-five minutes, rotating it slowly, ensuring
even heating.

The aroma---something none of them had ever smelled before---filled the
room.

The three men watched with widened eyes.

"This... this is real cooking..." Tanaka murmured.

After the fire settled, Kael extinguished the flames safely and stood
up.

He called out to everyone.

"It's ready."
