# **Chapter 7**

**Food Distribution Hub, Osaka Ruins \| September 11, 2120, 06:00**

Morning came quietly.\
Kael woke earlier than he expected, even though he'd fallen asleep well
past midnight.

Cherry was curled up in his arms, her soft white fur warm against his
skin. When he moved, she stirred---eyes half-open, blinking up at him
sleepily.

Kael chuckled. "Hey there, sleepyhead."

He glanced toward the horizon. Dawn had come, but the sky looked more
like dusk. A deep golden-red light bled across the clouds, the
reflection of the fires still raging far in the distance. The land
itself seemed to glow with a restless burn.

Kael's voice turned serious. "Iris, we need to cross the Yodo River
soon. If the fire keeps spreading at this rate, it'll reach here in a
few days. It's moving outward---slow, but steady."

Iris's voice resonated from his wrist, calm yet grave. "You're right.
The flames are devouring everything---buildings, soil, even stone. From
my analysis, the ground itself seems to be burning. The faster we cross,
the better our chances."

Cherry meowed softly, drawing Kael's attention. He blinked and frowned.
"Wait a second\... Iris, doesn't Cherry look---bigger than yesterday?"

Iris paused for a brief scan. "Yes. Her body mass has increased
significantly. Yesterday she appeared about two months old. Now her size
matches a four-month-old."

Kael's eyebrows rose. "That fast, huh?"

"And Kael," Iris added, "your injuries have also improved far beyond
normal healing speed. The lacerations on your chest and arm are nearly
closed. Based on your vitals, you'll be fully healed in two days. Your
overall strength has also increased---likely due to the residual energy
absorbed from the mutated dog."

Kael flexed his arm and realized she was right. The pain had dulled,
replaced by a faint warmth under his skin.

Then Iris shared the report directly to his mind:

**Kael Ardyn -- IRIS Status Report**\
**Age:** 21\
**Time Post-Awakening:** 1 Month\
**Location:** Food Distribution Hub, Osaka Ruins\
Date: September 11, 2120 \| Time: 06:15

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Core Attribute**             **Value**
  ------------------------------ ----------------------------------------
  Strength                       **1.80** (from 1.60)

  Speed                          **1.56** (from 1.26)

  Life Force                     **1.90** (from 1.70)

  Stamina                        **1.90** (from 1.70)

  Constitution                   **1.57** (from 1.37)

  Reaction                       **0.26s** (unchanged)
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

"Iris," Kael said slowly, "let's run a check on Cherry too. I want to
know what's happening with her."

"Understood."

Tiny components unfolded from Iris's watch form. A small syringe
extended and pricked Cherry gently. She didn't even flinch---just looked
curiously at Kael as the device withdrew. Moments later, the data
appeared in his mind.

**Cherry -- IRIS Status Report**\
**Age:** 2 Months\
**Time Post-Awakening:** 1 Month\
**Location:** Food Distribution Hub, Osaka Ruins\
Date: September 11, 2120 \| Time: 06:15

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Core Attribute**               **Value**
  -------------------------------- --------------------------------------
  Strength                         **2.04** (from 1.65)

  Speed                            **8.59** (from 8.24)

  Life Force                       **1.80** (from 1.20)

  Stamina                          **1.80** (from 1.20)

  Constitution                     **1.55** (from 1.15)
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

Kael's jaw tightened slightly. "Hold on... Cherry's strength is higher
than mine? How is that even possible? We ate the same food."

Iris answered in her usual calm tone. "First, Cherry consumed a larger
quantity than you. Second, she's still a young organism---growth at her
stage is naturally accelerated. And third..."

Kael groaned before she could finish. "Don't tell me---she was already
stronger than me to begin with."

"Exactly," Iris confirmed.

Kael sighed, leaning back. "Great. Outdone by a two-month-old cat."

Cherry tilted her head and meowed proudly, as if agreeing.

Kael couldn't help but smile. "Yeah, yeah. You win, Cherry."

Still, deep down, he felt a quiet reassurance. If Cherry was growing
stronger this fast, maybe---just maybe---they had a real chance at
surviving what was coming.

Iris's voice echoed softly from Kael's wrist.\
"Kael, prepare your gear. It's time to move toward the river."

Kael tightened the straps of his worn pack and looked around the Hub one
last time. "Yeah," he muttered. "No point staying here any longer."

Cherry perched neatly on his shoulder, tail swaying, eyes alert as
always.

Iris spoke again, calm and strategic. "Head through the open streets
with fewer buildings. Fewer structures mean fewer places for mutated
creatures---or people---to hide."

Kael gave a short nod. "Got it."

He set out, moving cautiously through the crumbling streets of Osaka.
The sky above was still tinged with red from distant fires, and ash
floated lazily in the air. What once had been a bustling district was
now silent---cars overturned, glass shattered, and every sound carried
too far.

The Yodo River was only eight kilometers away, but with the growing
number of mutants, the trip would take hours. Kael had to stay slow,
deliberate, and quiet.

After barely an hour, the first attack came.

A cluster of mutated beetles scuttled out from a cracked sewer
line---each the size of a human fist, their shells black and gleaming.
They clattered against the concrete, lunging in sudden bursts. Kael
swung his spear in quick arcs, breaking through their hard carapaces one
after another. When the last one split apart with a crunch, he stepped
back, breathing hard.

"Beetles," he muttered. "They're getting faster."

"They're also more aggressive," Iris replied. "Their muscle density has
doubled since pre-Awakening insects. Keep your distance next time. Use
the spear's reach."

Kael nodded silently and moved on.

Not long after, he ducked into a half-collapsed medical shop. The
shelves were mostly empty, but a few things still remained---scattered
bandages, a cracked med kit, some nutrient pods sealed in reinforced
glass. He brushed off the dust and packed everything usable.

"Iris, check the pods."

"Analyzing," she said, scanning through his wrist device. "Still stable.
Two are for stamina regeneration, one for tissue repair. Keep them
safe---you may need them soon."

"Noted."

Back outside, he followed a quieter street that led toward the river.
But peace didn't last long.

Iris alerted him immediately, "Kael there is a presence nearby."

A shadow darted across a broken wall---a mutated cat, much larger than
Cherry, muscles rippling under its dark fur. Its eyes glowed faint red,
fangs bared as it hissed low.

Kael froze. He didn't want to fight it. "Iris," he said under his
breath, "I'd rather avoid killing it. Might make Cherry uneasy."

But Cherry's reaction was the opposite. The small cat on his shoulder
arched her back and hissed violently, fur standing on end. Before Kael
could react, she nearly leapt off his shoulder toward the other cat.

"Whoa, easy!" He grabbed her just in time. "Hey! Calm down!"

Iris's voice cut in. "Kael, if you hesitate, it'll attack first."

Kael clenched his jaw, lowering Cherry gently behind a wall. Then he
faced the beast head-on. When it lunged, he sidestepped and drove the
spear clean through its neck. It hit the ground with a guttural snarl
before falling still.

He stood there for a moment, breathing heavily. "Damn it. Didn't want to
do that."

Cherry watched quietly from behind the car, her anger gone---only calm
acceptance in her eyes.

"You're doing well," Iris said. "Another hour, and we'll reach the
riverbank. Rest for five minutes, then move. I'll monitor the area."

Kael rested for a few minutes, catching his breath before wiped the
blood from his weapon and pressing on.

One hours later, the streets narrowed again.

The air was still heavy with ash, each step crunching faintly over
shattered glass and broken pavement. He had barely crossed two city
blocks when Iris's voice suddenly sharpened in his ear.

"Kael, stop!"

He froze instantly, spear halfway raised.

"What is it?"

"I'm detecting movement ahead," Iris said. "A group of five people. I
can't determine if they're mutated or not from this range. I'll need you
to get closer for a clearer reading."

Kael's heart skipped. "People?" he whispered.

"Yes," Iris confirmed. "But Kael..." Her tone softened, edged with
warning. "Remember---human nature can't always be trusted. A friend
today can turn against you tomorrow, especially when survival and
resources are at stake. We'll observe them first from a distance. If
they seem stable, we'll consider contact."

Kael nodded silently, mind steadying. *Hope and caution... always
together now.*

He moved quietly through the street, hugging the walls as Iris guided
him mentally. The signal led to a retail store---one of the automated
outlets that used to run entirely on AI systems before the collapse. The
glass front was cracked, displays long dead, shelves looted.

"Iris," he whispered, crouching behind a fallen sign, "this the place?"

"Yes. Inside that building. Five heat signatures---three males, two
females. From my analysis, two of the males are adults, one is younger.
Among the females, one adult and one child. They don't appear mutated...
but stay alert."

Kael edged forward until he reached a window. He peeked through a narrow
gap.

Inside, the dim interior flickered with faint daylight from the broken
ceiling. Three figures sat on the floor---an adult woman cradling a
small girl in her arms, the child looked weary but the mother held her
with a determined face. Nearby, two men rummaged through the store
shelves, tossing aside empty containers and broken equipment in search
of nutrient pods.

Kael's eyes lingered on the group, measuring their movements, their
condition... and then froze.

Among them, sitting quietly beside the woman, was a familiar face---a
man in his twenties with a shock of vibrant, unnatural blue hair falling
over his eyes, clad in a neon-trimmed windbreaker and cargo pants.

For a moment, Kael thought his mind was playing tricks on him. But
no---there was no mistake.\
It was someone he knew.\
Someone from the same college.\
*Hirota.*

His chest tightened. A classmate---from Osaka Metaverse College.\
He hadn't seen a familiar face in over a month.

Cherry shifted on his shoulder, sensing his sudden change in emotion,
and let out a quiet mewl. Kael placed a finger to her lips.

"Iris," he spoke mentally, "that's someone I know."

Iris paused briefly before replying, her tone calm but edged with
warning.\
"Then be careful, Kael. Familiarity doesn't always mean safety. But if
they turn hostile, remember---you and Cherry are far stronger than
ordinary humans. You can handle yourselves."

Kael inhaled deeply, steadying his heartbeat. He brushed the dust from
his coat, adjusted his grip on the spear, and stepped from behind the
wall.

The door of the shop creaked as he approached. Inside, the woman holding
her child was the first to see him. Her eyes widened.\
"Who's there?!" she shouted.

The two men reacted instantly. One older---mid-fifties, gray hair, face
hardened by exhaustion---raised a *pulse gun*. Its faint hum lit his
hands blue. The other, bulkier looked above thirty, lifted an electric
baton, its coils sparking faintly.

Kael froze.\
*Pulse gun?*\
He hadn't seen a functioning one since before the world fell apart. For
a second, his mind blanked. *That thing could kill me in an instant.*

He forced his voice steady. "My name's Kael Ardyn," he said slowly,
lifting his hand in surrender while keeping the spear down. "I mean no
harm. I saw your group and thought maybe we could help each other."

The group didn't lower their weapons. The air between them was
heavy---fragile, one breath away from chaos. Then the woman's eyes
darted to his shoulder.

Her face twisted. "A cat!!!---what is that thing?!" she hissed, raising
her gun again.

Kael's pulse spiked. "No! Don't shoot!" he blurted out, stepping back.
"She's not dangerous! She's with me!"

Cherry hissed sharply, fur bristling, a faint blue glint flickering in
her eyes.

Iris's voice rang inside Kael's mind. *Kael, their hostility level is
rising. Prepare to disengage. Use Cherry to create a distraction if
necessary---*

Before she could finish, a sudden shout cut through the tension.

"Stop!!"

All eyes turned.

A young man stepped forward, holding the small girl protectively behind
him. His face was smudged with dirt and fatigue, but his eyes---sharp,
electric blue beneath messy hair---locked onto Kael with disbelief.

"Kael... is that really you?"

Kael blinked, his heart thudding in his chest. He let his grip on the
spear loosen just slightly and pulled a faint, almost hesitant
smile---half genuine, half guarded.

"You..." he said, forcing surprise into his tone as if testing the
words. "You're... Hirota? Hirota Ren?"
