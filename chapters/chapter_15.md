# **Chapter 15**

**Abandoned Library \| September 11, 2120, 22:00**

A dead silence filled the library.

Everyone was still trying to process everything that had happened over
the last hour. The bridge crossing, the mutated crows, and finally
Cherry\'s overwhelming display of strength. Even after hearing Kael\'s
explanation, the image of the small white cat tearing through seven
mutated crows in mere seconds refused to leave their minds.

Hirota was the first to break the silence.

\"If Cherry is a friendly mutant\... doesn\'t that mean there might be
other mutated animals that won\'t harm us?\"

The question drew everyone\'s attention.

He looked around before continuing.

\"There might even be mutated animals the **Sovereign Defense
Directorate** could tame\... or train to help people.\"

Nakamura rested his chin on his hand, giving the idea some thought.

\"It isn\'t impossible.\"

Tanaka nodded slowly.

\"The world has changed far beyond anything we imagined. We can\'t judge
everything using the standards of the old world anymore.\"

Emily looked down at Cherry, who was quietly resting beside Kael.

\"Cherry never hurt anyone.\"

She gently scratched behind Cherry\'s ears.

\"She even saved us.\"

Cherry responded with a soft purr, closing her eyes in satisfaction.

Hirota let out a small sigh.

\"Well\... as long as she doesn\'t harm us, that\'s all that really
matters, right?\"

Everyone\'s gaze unconsciously shifted toward Maya.

She remained silent for several moments, looking first at Cherry and
then at the sleeping Emily leaning against her shoulder.

Finally, she nodded.

\"\...Yes.\"

Her expression softened slightly.

\"As long as she\'s still the Cherry Emily loves\... I don\'t have a
reason to fear her.\"

The atmosphere inside the library finally relaxed.

Nakamura clapped his hands once, breaking the lingering silence.

\"Alright.\"

Everyone looked toward him.

\"Now that we\'ve settled this, let\'s focus on something far more
important.\"

He slowly rolled his aching shoulders before continuing.

\"Rest.\"

\"And recovery.\"

\"The journey here has exhausted all of us, both physically and
mentally. We\'ve been running, fighting, and surviving with barely any
proper sleep.\"

His eyes swept across the room.

\"If we keep pushing ourselves like this, we\'ll collapse before any
mutant manages to kill us.\"

A few weary smiles appeared around the room.

\"Tonight, we recover.\"

Nobody objected.

The past several days had pushed every one of them to their absolute
limit.

Using the portable stove and cookware they had brought along, the group
spent the next half hour preparing dinner from the roasted mutated meat
they had gathered during their journey. The rich aroma gradually filled
the reading hall, replacing the stale smell of dust and old books.

Emily sat beside Cherry, watching the meat cook over the small flame.

\"I think this smells much better than those nutrient pods.\"

\"It definitely does,\" Hirota replied with a tired laugh. \"I\'m never
eating another synthetic meal if I survive this.\"

Tanaka chuckled softly.

\"I never thought mutant meat would become gourmet food.\"

Even Maya couldn\'t help smiling.

Cherry\'s nose twitched continuously toward the roasting meat.

Kael noticed immediately.

\"Hungry?\"

Cherry looked up at him.

\"\...Meow.\"

\"I\'ll take that as a yes.\"

The small exchange drew another round of quiet laughter.

It wasn\'t much.

But after everything they had endured\...

It felt nice.

When the meat was finally ready, everyone ate quietly. The warmth of the
freshly roasted meat eased some of the fatigue weighing on their bodies.
It wasn\'t enough to fully restore their strength, but it was enough to
make them feel alive again.

After dinner, they organized sleeping places inside one of the secured
reading halls.

Nakamura carefully inspected every entrance twice before finally
allowing himself to rest.

Maya spread blankets across one corner of the room before gently helping
Emily lie down.

Tanaka settled against a nearby wall, his eyes already beginning to
close.

Hirota barely managed to find a place before collapsing onto the floor
with an exhausted groan.

Kael chose a spot beside one of the reinforced windows.

Outside, the dense mutated forest surrounding the library swayed gently
beneath the cool night breeze.

Cherry quietly jumped into his lap.

\"You tired too?\" Kael asked softly.

Cherry answered with a wide yawn before curling into a small white ball.

Kael gently stroked her back.

\"Good work today.\"

Cherry purred quietly without opening her eyes.

Inside his mind, Iris spoke in her usual calm tone.

\"No hostile biological signatures detected within the current scan
radius.\"

Kael gave a faint nod.

\"Then\... let\'s rest while we can.\"

One by one, everyone drifted to sleep.

The library slowly fell silent once again.

The next morning\...

Soft rays of sunlight filtered through the library windows, casting long
beams across the quiet reading hall.

Kael slowly opened his eyes.

For the first time in what felt like an eternity, he had slept through
an entire night without being woken by the sound of fighting.

Cherry was still asleep beside him, curled into a small white ball.

The peaceful atmosphere lasted only a few seconds.

\"EVERYONE!\"

Hirota\'s panicked shout echoed through the library.

\"COME HERE! There\'s\... there\'s something outside!\"

The sudden uproar jolted everyone awake.

Nakamura immediately reached for his electric baton while Maya
instinctively pulled the half-awake Emily into her arms.

Kael rushed toward the window.

The moment he looked outside---

\"\...What?\"

Everyone froze.

The scenery beyond the reinforced glass had completely changed.

The dozens of towering trees that had surrounded the library the
previous night had vanished.

In their place stood a single gigantic tree.

To call it enormous would have been an understatement.

It towered nearly thirty to forty meters into the sky, its massive
canopy spreading over the surrounding area like a green ocean. The trunk
alone was wider than half the library building, its bark resembling the
walls of an ancient fortress rather than the surface of a tree.

Its roots spread across the ground like enormous serpents, disappearing
beneath the earth in every direction.

Standing before it, the library looked insignificant.

It was as though a fragment of an ancient world had appeared overnight,
replacing everything that had existed before.

Nobody spoke.

Even after witnessing mutated animals, impossible strength, and a
collapsing civilization\...

This was beyond anything they could comprehend.

Tanaka gripped the window frame tightly.

\"\...How\...\"

Hirota simply stared.

\"Where did all the other trees go?\"

No one had an answer.

Kael\'s eyes never left the colossal tree.

\"Iris\...\"

He spoke aloud without even realizing it.

\"Do you have any idea what we\'re looking at?\"

Normally, Iris answered without hesitation.

This time\...

She remained silent for nearly two seconds.

When she finally spoke, there was something Kael had never heard before.

Uncertainty.

\"I possess no data capable of explaining this phenomenon.\"

Everyone unconsciously looked toward Kael as he continued listening.

\"Even assuming the environmental mutation rate increased by several
thousand times beyond its current projection, the instantaneous growth
of a lifeform to this scale remains statistically impossible.\"

A brief pause followed.

\"I am unable to perform a complete biological analysis.\"

Kael frowned.

\"You can\'t?\"

\"No.\"

Her voice remained calm, yet noticeably slower than usual.

\"Ordinarily, I can detect the life force emitted by plant organisms.\"

Another pause.

\"But from that tree\...\"

\"I detect nothing.\"

Kael\'s expression stiffened.

\"No life force?\"

\"It is as though\...\"

Iris hesitated for the briefest moment.

\"\...there is no living organism standing before us.\"

A chill ran through the room.

Tanaka continued staring through the window, his voice barely above a
whisper.

\"How is something like this possible\...?\"

He slowly shook his head.

\"Can the mutation spreading across the world really create something
like that overnight?\"

Hirota pushed open another window slightly, unable to take his eyes off
the colossal tree.

\"I think we should leave.\"

Everyone turned toward him.

\"Staying beside\... whatever that thing is\... doesn\'t feel safe.\"

Maya immediately disagreed.

\"No.\"

She adjusted Emily, who was still waking up in her arms.

\"It isn\'t safe for us to move either.\"

She looked around at everyone.

\"Kael and Tanaka are still injured. Every one of us is exhausted after
escaping death several times and crossing that bridge.\"

She gave a small, bitter smile.

\"My arms and legs still hurt whenever I move.\"

Nakamura nodded silently.

She was right.

Kael thought for a moment before speaking.

\"I agree.\"

Everyone looked toward him.

\"This library is one of the safest places we\'ve found so far.\"

He glanced back at the giant tree.

\"The building is reinforced, we have supplies, and there dosn't seem to
be any mutated creatures in the surrounding.\"

He continued calmly.

\"We\'ll remain here for now.\"

\"If we recover our strength or establish contact with the **Regional
Joint Command**, we\'ll decide our next move.\"

His gaze returned to the tree outside.

\"But until then\...\"

\"We monitor that tree every day.\"

\"If anything changes, we respond immediately.\"

Nobody objected.

For now\...

Waiting was the safest option.

Not long afterward, Emily finally woke completely.

She rubbed her sleepy eyes before looking through the window.

\"\...Whoa\...\"

Her mouth slowly fell open.

\"When did that tree get here?\"

Maya gently explained everything that had happened while she was still
asleep.

Emily listened quietly before hugging Cherry a little tighter.

The morning soon settled into a routine.

Maya and Hirota prepared breakfast using the remaining library rations
along with the roasted mutated meat they had stored. Following Kael\'s
suggestions, they carefully rationed both the nutrient pods and the meat
to make their supplies last as long as possible.

Meanwhile, Nakamura and Tanaka searched every available communication
system inside the library.

Emergency transmitters.

Library communication terminals.

Even the old administrative network.

Nothing worked.

Every external connection remained dead.

Only the library\'s internal automated systems continued operating
through their local network.

After breakfast, Kael and Hirota explored the building together.

The library consisted of a single large floor divided into rows of
neatly arranged bookshelves. At one corner was a small staff room that
had once belonged to the librarians, connected to a charging station for
the automated service robots where they had prepared their meals
earlier.

Instead of traditional books, most of the library relied on digital
learning stations. Small projectors and interactive terminals allowed
visitors to read books or access educational material directly from the
city\'s network before the disaster.

Beneath the main floor was a compact underground storage room.

It contained several preserved energy pods, nutrient solutions,
emergency supplies, and a collection of carefully stored printed books
from before complete digitalization.

It was also where Nakamura continued attempting to establish contact
with the **Regional Joint Command** using the library\'s specialized
emergency communication equipment.

Three days passed.

The library remained as quiet as the day they had arrived.

Not a single mutated animal had appeared during those three days. Even
the surrounding forest remained eerily still, as if every living
creature had instinctively avoided the area around the gigantic tree.

The silence itself had become unsettling.

Fortunately, the peaceful days allowed everyone to recover.

With Maya\'s treatment and the constant supply of roasted mutated meat,
both Kael and Tanaka had fully recovered from their injuries. The others
also felt the changes within their bodies. Every meal of mutated meat
gradually strengthened them, making their movements lighter and their
bodies sturdier than before.

Iris quietly displayed the latest status report before Kael.

The three peaceful days allowed the group to slowly adapt to their new
reality.

Nakamura and Tanaka continued trying to establish contact with the
**Regional Joint Command** whenever possible, refusing to give up
despite repeated failures.

Kael spent much of his time training and studying the subtle changes in
his body under Iris\'s guidance.

Maya managed the group\'s meals and medical supplies, while Hirota often
helped organize the library and search through its old records.

Whenever they had nothing else to do, they simply read the preserved
books to distract themselves from the uncertainty outside.

Only Emily seemed to have found a daily routine.

Every morning, she carried Cherry to one of the reinforced windows
overlooking the enormous tree.

The giant tree hadn\'t changed at all during the past three days.

It stood there in complete silence, towering over the surrounding
landscape like an ancient guardian.

Emily gently hugged Cherry as both of them stared through the thick
glass.

\"Cherry\...\"

She looked up at the massive canopy stretching across the sky.

\"Do you think that tree is like the other mutated animals?\"

Her voice became quieter.

\"Do you think it\'ll try to kill us if it notices we\'re here?\"

Cherry tilted her head.

\"\...Meow.\"

Emily smiled faintly.

\"I hope you\'re right.\"

Almost as if responding to their conversation---

The countless leaves covering the gigantic tree suddenly began rustling.

Emily blinked.

For the first time since they had arrived\...

There was no wind.

Yet the leaves continued swaying violently.

Even stranger---

one of the enormous branches high above slowly began to move on its own.

It wasn\'t swaying with the tree.

It was moving.

Under the watchful eyes of Emily and Cherry, the colossal branch
continued its slow, unnatural motion.
