# Chapter 16

**Abandoned Library \| September 15, 2120, 8:20**

Before Cherry could react---

A gigantic branch shot through the reinforced windows and entrance of
the library like a spear.

The thick glass shattered.

Steel supports bent inward as if they were paper.

In less than a heartbeat, the branch coiled around Emily\'s petite body
and lifted her into the air.

\"Ahhh!\"

Emily\'s terrified scream echoed throughout the library.

Cherry reacted instantly.

The white cat launched herself toward the branch, her speed turning into
nothing more than a white blur.

Less than 0.2 seconds had passed since the attack began.

Cherry landed on the branch beside Emily and dug her claws into the bark
with all her strength.

*Clang.*

A sharp metallic sound rang out.

Her claws, capable of slicing apart mutated crows in an instant, failed
to leave even the slightest scratch on the tree.

Not even a single leaf fell.

The violent crash had already alerted everyone inside the library.

Kael, Nakamura, Maya, Hirota, and Tanaka rushed toward the entrance.

The sight before them froze everyone in place.

Several enormous branches had pierced through the reinforced walls and
windows, filling the library like the roots of a monstrous beast.

Emily struggled helplessly as the branch tightened around her body.

Cherry was attacking with everything she had.

Yet the seemingly invincible white cat couldn\'t even damage a single
leaf.

It was completely illogical.

\"Emily!\"

Maya\'s scream broke the silence.

Without hesitation, she grabbed the pulse gun from the nearby table and
fired repeatedly at the branches.

Blue pulses struck the bark one after another.

Nothing happened.

Nakamura charged forward with his electric baton.

Kael thrust his makeshift spear with all his strength.

Hirota joined them, striking the branch with a metal pipe.

Tanaka immediately activated the library\'s intrusion protocol.

Twenty maintenance robots emerged from their charging stations and
rushed toward the branches, repeatedly smashing them with their
mechanical arms.

Every attack landed.

None of them mattered.

Not a scratch.

Not even the smallest crack.

The giant tree didn\'t even attempt to defend itself.

It simply remained motionless, as though the combined attacks of
everyone inside the library were no more bothersome than falling rain.

Kael gritted his teeth as he drove his spear forward again.

\"Iris!\"

He barely stopped attacking.

\"Do you have any plan that can save Emily?\"

There was a brief silence before Iris answered.

\"\...No.\"

Even her normally unwavering voice sounded heavier.

\"Nothing within our current capabilities can damage this organism.\"

Another strike from Kael\'s spear bounced away uselessly.

\"Since the attack began, it has not retaliated even once. From a
logical standpoint, the highest probability of survival is immediate
evacuation before it decides to target the remaining survivors.\"

Kael clenched his jaw.

\"You already know my answer.\"

\"\...Yes.\"

Iris replied quietly.

\"I know you will continue trying to save the little girl.\"

Kael let out a bitter smile despite the situation.

\"If I abandoned her now\...\"

His spear struck the branch once more.

\"\...I\'d spend the rest of my life regretting it.\"

The group continued attacking relentlessly.

One minute.

Five minutes.

Ten minutes.

The robots gradually slowed as their batteries neared exhaustion.

Finally---

One after another, they shut down completely.

Silence returned.

Then\...

The giant tree moved again.

Before anyone could react, another branch shot forward with frightening
speed.

It wrapped around Kael.

At the same instant, another branch seized Cherry.

The white cat struggled violently as she was pulled upward beside Emily.

Using her free forelegs, Cherry unleashed a flurry of slashes against
the branch restraining Emily.

Again.

Again.

Again.

Her claws moved so quickly they became invisible. Each slash faster than
before.

Yet the branch remained completely unharmed.

Then---

The gigantic tree finally responded.

Its colossal trunk slowly bent downward.

Forty meters of ancient wood leaned toward Cherry until the enormous
trunk stopped directly before her.

The pressure that descended upon the surroundings was indescribable.

Everyone froze.

Nobody dared move.

Even Cherry instinctively stopped attacking.

Emily\'s crying ceased.

An overwhelming presence filled the air.

It wasn\'t murderous.

Nor was it hostile.

It was simply\...

Absolute.

As though they stood before a being far beyond their understanding.

Several seconds passed.

Without warning, the branch holding Emily loosened.

She dropped gently onto the ground.

\"Emily!\"

Maya immediately ran forward and caught her daughter.

At the same moment, the gigantic tree began sinking into the earth.

Its immense roots disappeared beneath the ground one after another.

The towering trunk slowly descended until only a massive crater remained
before the library.

The impossible tree\...

Had vanished.

Only then did everyone regain control over their bodies.

Hirota stared blankly at the enormous crater.

\"\...What just happened?\"

Nakamura looked around in disbelief.

\"Where did the tree go?\"

Maya held Emily tightly in her arms, tears streaming down her face.

\"My baby\...\"

She checked Emily from head to toe with trembling hands.

\"Emily!\"

\"I\'m\... I\'m here, Mom.\"

Emily buried her face into Maya\'s shoulder and cried.

Maya hugged her even tighter.

\"You\'re safe\...\"

\"You\'re safe\...\"

Hirota suddenly looked around frantically.

\"If Emily is here\...\"

His face turned pale.

\"\...then where\'s Kael?\"

\"And Cherry?\"

The three men searched the destroyed library and the surrounding area.

Every room.

Every corner.

Every collapsed section of the entrance.

Nothing.

Several minutes later, Nakamura slowly lowered his head.

\"They\'re gone.\"

Tanaka closed his eyes.

\"The giant tree\...\"

\"\...took Kael and Cherry with it.\"

Hirota\'s legs gave out beneath him.

He collapsed onto the floor.

\"\...Why?\"

His voice trembled.

\"Why\...\"

Tears streamed down his face.

\"Why does everyone close to me disappear one after another?\"

He struck the floor with his fist.

\"Am I a curse\...?\"

\"Do I only bring death to my friends\...\"

\"\...and my family?\"

Nakamura placed a hand on Hirota\'s shoulder.

\"It isn\'t your fault.\"

His voice remained firm.

\"We don\'t know if they\'re dead.\"

He looked toward the enormous crater outside.

\"The tree never attacked us after taking them.\"

\"That means\...\"

\"\...there\'s still a chance Kael and Cherry are alive.\"

Tanaka nodded.

\"You have to survive.\"

He looked directly at Hirota.

\"If Kael risked his life to protect everyone here\...\"

\"Then the least we can do is continue living.\"

\"Whether he returns or not\...\"

\"We survive.\"

Silence filled the ruined library once again.

No one knew where Kael and Cherry had gone.

No one knew whether they would ever return.

With another attack always possible, the group made a difficult
decision.

They would recover as quickly as possible\...

And leave the library.

Waiting any longer beside that mysterious giant tree was no longer an
option.

Meanwhile, hundreds of meters beneath the ground\...

Kael slowly regained consciousness.

An unbearable heat pressed against his body, making every breath feel
heavy. The air itself felt suffocating, as though there wasn\'t enough
oxygen to fill his lungs.

A sharp ringing echoed continuously inside his ears.

\"\...Kael\...\"

The voice sounded distant.

\"\...Wake up\...\"

His eyelids twitched.

\"\...Kael! Wake up!\"

Kael slowly opened his eyes.

A violent wave of dizziness struck him immediately, forcing him to shut
them again. He remained still for several seconds until the spinning
sensation gradually subsided.

Only then did the ringing begin to fade.

\"Yeah\... Iris.\"

He slowly rubbed the back of his head as he sat up.

\"I\'m awake\... just a little dizzy.\"

Memories flooded back all at once.

The giant tree.

Emily being captured.

Cherry attacking the branches.

That overwhelming presence\...

And finally\...

Darkness.

Kael\'s expression stiffened.

\"The tree\...\"

He immediately looked around.

\"What happened to everyone?\"

\"Iris\...\"

His voice grew serious.

\"Did that giant tree bring me underground?\"

\"Correct.\"

Iris answered without hesitation.

\"We are currently beneath the surface.\"

\"Although I cannot accurately determine our depth, calculations based
on the surrounding temperature and geological pressure estimate we are
several hundred meters underground.\"

She paused briefly.

\"However\...\"

\"The surrounding environment is highly anomalous.\"

\"It resembles a cave constructed from stone.\"

\"But my scans indicate the entire structure is composed of wood.\"

Kael frowned.

\"Wood?\"

Only now did he properly observe his surroundings.

He was sitting on what looked like a woven platform made entirely of
thick tree branches.

The walls curved around him like a gigantic nest, enclosing the entire
space without a single visible entrance or exit.

Soft streams of pale green light glowed from within the wooden walls,
illuminating the chamber just enough for him to see.

Everything around him was alive.

Fresh bark.

Living branches.

Not a single piece showed signs of drying or decay.

Even under the intense underground heat, the wood remained vibrant and
moist.

\"I understand why you called it impossible\...\"

Kael murmured.

\"No ordinary wood should remain alive under these conditions.\"

\"Correct.\"

Iris continued.

\"Based on all known biological data, wood exposed to temperatures and
pressures at this depth should have dried, decayed, or carbonized.\"

\"Yet every scan indicates the surrounding structure remains
biologically fresh.\"

Kael slowly stood.

His hand pressed against one of the walls.

The surface felt warm.

Almost\...

Alive.

His eyes searched every corner of the chamber.

Nothing.

No openings.

No tunnels.

No cracks.

Just an enormous wooden cocoon buried hundreds of meters beneath the
earth.

A heavy silence settled around him.

\"\...Does this mean I\'m trapped?\"

He slowly looked upward despite knowing there was nothing to see beyond
the wooden ceiling.

\"Hundreds of meters underground\...\"

\"No way out.\"

\"No idea where I am.\"

His breathing became heavier.

\"So\...\"

\"I\'m supposed to wait here until something comes to kill me?\"

For the first time since the disaster began, Kael felt truly helpless.

Every challenge until now had offered some path forward.

Fight. Run. Hide. Survive.

But here\...

There wasn\'t even a direction to move.

Suddenly another thought crossed his mind.

\"Cherry!\"

He immediately turned around.

\"Where\'s Cherry?\"

\"She was captured too.\"

\"Did the tree bring her here as well?\"

Iris answered almost immediately.

\"No.\"

\"I have continuously scanned the surrounding area since you regained
consciousness.\"

\"I detected no biological signatures besides your own.\"

Kael frowned.

\"No life signs?\"

\"None.\"

She continued calmly.

\"I cannot even detect life force from the surrounding wooden
structure.\"

\"Based on observations made during the encounter, the probability that
Cherry has been placed inside another chamber similar to this one is
considerably high.\"

Kael leaned against the wooden wall.

\"\...So we\'re separated.\"

\"Correct.\"

A brief silence followed before Iris spoke again.

\"There is another possibility.\"

Kael looked up.

\"What possibility?\"

\"The giant tree displayed no hostile intent throughout the encounter.\"

Kael remained quiet.

\"It restrained everyone.\"

\"It ignored every attack.\"

\"It released Emily unharmed.\"

\"It only departed after taking you and Cherry.\"

Iris continued analyzing.

\"Based on these observations, I hypothesize that the tree became aware
of our presence shortly after we arrived at the library.\"

\"It likely revealed itself because of an interest in us.\"

Kael blinked.

\"\...Interest?\"

\"For the following four days, the library remained sealed.\"

\"We never approached it.\"

\"Therefore, another possible explanation is that the tree eventually
decided to retrieve the objects of its interest itself.\"

Silence filled the chamber.

Kael stared blankly at the glowing wooden wall before letting out a
helpless laugh.

\"Iris\...\"

\"\...that\'s one absurd hypothesis.\"

Silence filled the chamber.

Several seconds passed.

\"It is.\"

The reply came.

Not inside his mind.

It came from somewhere in front of him.

Kael\'s pupils contracted.

A chill ran down his spine.

The voice was calm.

Gentle, even.

Yet beneath that gentleness lay an indescribable authority, as though
every word naturally demanded to be heard.

Kael immediately sprang to his feet, turning in every direction as his
eyes searched for the source of the voice.

Iris spoke to him.

\"I detected an increase in your heart rate, elevated stress hormones,
and sudden muscle tension.\"

\"You appear frightened.\"

Kael swallowed.

\"The voice.\"

\"I just heard someone speak.\"

\"No external sound waves have been detected.\"

Iris answered without hesitation.

\"My auditory sensors have registered no voice other than yours.\"

Before Kael could respond---

\"You needn\'t concern yourself with the little device upon your
wrist.\"

The voice returned.

This time, the overwhelming pressure from before was gone.

It sounded\... almost conversational.

\"Only you can hear me.\"

Kael instinctively took a step backward.

\"Who are you?\"

His voice remained cautious.

\"Why did you bring me here?\"

\"Kael?\"

Iris spoke immediately.

\"Who are you communicating with?\"

Kael never took his eyes off the empty chamber.

\"I think\...\"

He slowly answered.

\"I\'m talking to that giant tree.\"

\"It somehow made it so only I can hear its voice.\"

A brief silence followed.

Then Iris spoke in her usual calm tone.

\"Current analysis updated.\"

\"I am detecting no abnormality in your neural activity that would
indicate hallucinations or cognitive impairment.\"

\"Therefore, the possibility of an unknown communication method must now
be considered.\"

Before Kael could answer---

The wooden floor began moving.

Branches slowly separated.

A tiny green sprout emerged from the center of the chamber.

Kael watched without blinking.

The sprout continued growing.

Seconds became minutes.

Its thin stem thickened.

New branches unfolded.

Bright green leaves bloomed one after another.

Dark bark spread across the trunk, turning nearly black as it matured.

Within only a few minutes\...

A tree nearly one and a half meters tall stood before him.

It possessed only a single fruit.

A perfect sphere roughly the size of a human fist.

Its smooth metallic-blue skin reflected the chamber\'s pale light like
polished chrome.

Thin neon-red veins covered its surface, pulsing rhythmically\...

Almost like a heartbeat.

A strange fragrance gradually filled the chamber.

Fresh rain.

The scent of ozone after lightning.

And\...

Somehow\...

The warm, comforting smell of Kael\'s happiest childhood memory.
