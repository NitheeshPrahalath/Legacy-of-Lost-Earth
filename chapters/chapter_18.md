# Chapter 18

**Underground wood Chamber \| September 15, 2120, 9:50**

\"Wake up.\"

The words did not travel through the air. They bloomed directly within
Kael\'s consciousness, carrying an impossible warmth that instantly
dissolved the darkness surrounding him. The voice was neither loud nor
demanding, yet it possessed an absolute authority that could not be
resisted. The heavy haze clouding his mind shattered, replaced by an
overwhelming urge to open his eyes and witness the owner of that voice.

The moment his eyelids parted, everything he understood about reality
ceased to exist.

He stood upon an enormous celestial body drifting silently through the
endless expanse of deep space. There was no atmosphere, no wind, no
sound. Countless stars glittered like scattered diamonds across the
infinite darkness while rivers of meteoroids drifted peacefully between
distant galaxies. Yet beneath his feet, life flourished. Ancient soil
nourished endless forests of towering emerald trees, crystal-clear
streams wound between rolling hills, and fields of vibrant flowers
stretched toward the horizon. Every leaf, every blade of grass, every
drop of water existed where life should have been impossible. It was a
living world suspended within the silent heart of the cosmos.

Then Kael slowly raised his eyes.

His breathing stopped.

At the very center of that celestial world stood a tree so unimaginably
colossal that the asteroid beneath it appeared no larger than a pebble
supporting a mountain. Its roots vanished deep into the heart of the
celestial body, while its trunk rose endlessly until it disappeared
beyond sight. Its countless branches stretched across the heavens like
rivers woven through the stars, embracing entire constellations beneath
their vast canopy.

Looking upon it felt\... wrong.

Every instinct within Kael screamed that a human was never meant to
witness something so immense. His mind desperately tried to comprehend
its size, only to recoil each time, as though his very consciousness
rejected an existence that surpassed all reason.

\"Did you sleep well, Kael?\"

The gentle voice returned.

It seemed to come from every direction at once, yet somehow it also felt
as though the colossal tree itself was speaking.

Kael opened his mouth, but no words came.

Before such an existence, language itself seemed meaningless.

A soft, melodious chuckle drifted through the endless silence.

\"It seems my existence is still too overwhelming for your mind to bear,
even within this shared illusion. We do not have much time, so I shall
speak only of what you must know.\"

The countless leaves covering the colossal tree shimmered softly,
bathing the entire celestial world in a warm emerald glow.

\"Your world has already begun to change. What humanity has witnessed
until now is merely the beginning. Across your world, ancient existences
that once slumbered beyond the flow of time have begun to awaken. Some
humans will perish in the days to come. Others will rise above them.\"

Kael remained silent, listening with unwavering attention.

\"I brought you here because I saw potential within you. Whether that
potential blossoms or fades depends entirely upon the path you choose.
The days ahead will demand more than strength. They will demand those
capable of standing before others when despair threatens to consume
them.\"

The enormous branches swayed gently.

For a brief moment, it felt as though the stars themselves moved with
them.

\"Remember this, Kael. Humanity believes its greatest enemies walk upon
the world beneath your feet. That belief is mistaken. One day, you will
understand that your world\'s true threat has never come from within,
but from beyond the heavens themselves.\"

The gentle voice became even softer.

\"Your body has reached its limit. Remaining here any longer will cause
irreversible harm. We shall meet again, Kael, when the time is right.\"

The stars slowly blurred.

The forests dissolved into countless particles of emerald light.

Even the colossal world-tree gradually faded from view until only
endless radiance remained.

\...

When Kael regained consciousness, he found himself lying on soft earth.

His vision was blurred, as though a veil covered his eyes, but he could
make out towering tree canopies swaying gently above him. Rays of
sunlight filtered through the leaves, painting shifting patches of light
across the forest floor.

The last thing he remembered was the unbearable heat deep beneath the
earth.

Slowly, he pushed himself up.

A dull ache spread through every muscle in his body. His limbs felt
unusually heavy, as though he had not moved them for days. Even
breathing required more effort than usual.

As his vision gradually cleared, he looked around.

He was surrounded by lush greenery.

Tall trees, each standing five to seven meters high, stretched in every
direction. Unlike the monstrous tree from before, these appeared
completely ordinary. The forest was peaceful, almost tranquil, as if
nothing extraordinary had ever happened.

Only then did Kael notice a familiar weight resting on his stomach.

Lowering his head, his eyes widened.

Cherry was curled into a small white ball, sleeping peacefully atop him.

\"Cherry!\"

He immediately wrapped his arms around her.

Startled awake, Cherry blinked several times before rubbing her head
against his chest.

She opened her mouth and meowed repeatedly.

Yet\...

Kael heard nothing.

His smile slowly faded.

A trace of panic crept into his eyes.

\"Cherry\...?\"

Her mouth continued moving, but not a single sound reached him.

Just then, Iris\'s familiar voice echoed inside his mind.

\"Kael\... Cherry is unharmed.\"

A brief pause followed.

\"The one who has temporarily lost the ability to hear\... is you.\"

Only then did Kael realize something else.

He couldn\'t hear his own breathing.

He couldn\'t hear his own voice.

\"Iris\... what happened underground? How did we escape? Did the giant
tree bring us back? And\... how did Cherry end up here?\"

His questions came one after another.

Iris remained silent for a moment before answering.

\"First, calm down.\"

\"Your body experienced severe physiological stress. It is currently
recovering, but several of your sensory and motor functions have not
fully returned. Your hearing is temporarily impaired, and your movements
remain noticeably slower than normal.\"

She paused briefly before continuing.

\"After you consumed the fruit, your body temperature continued rising
beyond the surrounding temperature. Eventually, your nervous system shut
down completely as a protective response.\"

\"I waited for the giant tree until the very end.\"

Another brief silence.

\"\...But it never appeared.\"

\"Or perhaps a more accurate statement would be that I have no data
explaining what happened afterward.\"

\"One moment we were surrounded by flames hundreds of meters beneath the
earth.\"

\"The next\...\"

\"\...we were here.\"

\"You were unconscious.\"

\"Cherry was asleep on top of you.\"

\"I detected no signs of movement, transportation, or any transition
between those two moments.\"

Kael lowered his gaze.

Neither of them spoke for several seconds.

\"Iris\...\"

\"I remember something.\"

\"I don\'t know whether it was a dream\... or something else.\"

He slowly recounted everything he had witnessed.

\"The place looked like the cosmos itself.\"

\"I was standing on an enormous asteroid, but it wasn\'t barren. Forests
covered it\... rivers flowed across it\... life existed where it
shouldn\'t have.\"

His expression gradually became solemn.

\"And at the center\...\"

\"There was a tree.\"

\"Just trying to remember its size makes my head hurt.\"

Instinctively, he pressed a hand against his forehead.

\"It spoke to me.\"

\"It said it had chosen me because I possessed the potential to lead
humanity.\"

\"It also warned me\...\"

\"\...that humanity\'s true enemy doesn\'t come from our world.\"

\"It comes from above.\"

Kael let out a slow breath.

\"I don\'t understand any of it.\"

For several moments, Iris remained completely silent.

Iris quietly organized every piece of information Kael had shared,
comparing it against everything stored within her database.

Just then, she paused.

\"\...Kael.\"

\"There has been a change.\"

\"What happened?\"

\"I have regained access to the global network.\"

Kael\'s eyes widened slightly.

Silence followed as Iris processed an overwhelming amount of
information.

Several seconds passed.

When she finally spoke again, even her usually composed voice carried an
unfamiliar heaviness.

\"Kael\... I think I understand what that voice meant.\"

\"The mutation is no longer limited to Japan.\"

\"It is spreading across the entire world.\"

Kael remained silent, allowing Iris to continue.

\"According to reports gathered from surviving governments, Defense
Organization communications, and scientific organizations, multiple
research expeditions were being conducted across various regions of the
planet before the catastrophe.\"

\"It appears that one or more of those expeditions triggered the
awakening of an ancient existence.\"

She paused briefly.

\"Or perhaps\... it would be more accurate to say they disturbed
something that should never have been awakened.\"

Kael unconsciously swallowed.

\"The awakening of that first Ancient Beast appears to have triggered a
chain reaction.\"

\"So far, twenty-seven Ancient Beasts have been confirmed across the
globe.\"

\"Each awakened in a different location.\"

\"As they emerged, every surrounding region suffered catastrophic
destruction.\"

Iris continued listing the reports.

\"Some regions were consumed by cyclones.\"

\"Others disappeared beneath tsunamis.\"

\"Some were engulfed in endless firestorms.\"

\"Others experienced earthquakes, poisonous miasma, or abnormal freezing
temperatures.\"

\"The minimum confirmed destruction radius surrounding each awakening
site exceeds one hundred kilometers.\"

\"The mutation is also strongest around these epicenters before
gradually spreading outward.\"

She projected Japan\'s information for Kael.

\"In Japan, the confirmed epicenter is Shingu, Wakayama Prefecture.\"

\"The affected region currently exceeds one hundred and twenty
kilometers.\"

\"Nara Prefecture and Wakayama Prefecture have suffered near-total
destruction.\"

\"Large portions of Mie Prefecture continue burning beneath uncontrolled
firestorms.\"

\"The situation throughout Osaka remains critical.\"

\"Awaji Island in Hyogo Prefecture has also been completely devastated.
Every major **Joint Base** within those regions has been lost. No
confirmed survivors have been reported.\"

\"The **Tenri Security Base** in Nara was also destroyed.\"

Kael stood frozen.

His fingers unconsciously tightened around Cherry\'s soft fur.

The information was far beyond anything he had imagined.

After a long silence, Iris spoke again.

\"\...Kael.\"

\"There is one more piece of information.\"

\"This may be the most important.\"

Her voice became noticeably quieter.

\"Current global survival estimates indicate that fewer than five
percent of humanity will survive the mutation.\"

Kael\'s breathing grew heavier.

\"For people living near an Ancient Beast\'s awakening site\...\"

\"The estimated survival rate falls below zero point two percent.\"

She paused before delivering the final statistic.

\"For those who were inside the epicenter itself\...\"

\"The estimated survival rate is below zero point zero five percent.\"

\"Even those fortunate enough to survive the mutation must still escape
regions overflowing with mutated creatures.\"

\"In reality\...\"

\"Very few of those survivors are expected to remain alive for long.\"

Silence returned to the forest.

After several moments, Iris spoke again.

\"Kael\... there is one positive development.\"

He looked up.

\"Those who survive the mutation appear to undergo a complete
enhancement of their bodies.\"

\"Strength, speed, stamina, constitution, and overall vitality all
increase significantly.\"

\"It matches the conclusions we reached through our own observations.\"

Kael nodded silently.

\"So\... everyone who survives becomes stronger.\"

\"Correct.\"

\"However, there is something else I noticed.\"

\"The information regarding the benefits of consuming mutated animal
meat has not appeared in any public reports.\"

Iris paused briefly.

\"I consider it highly unlikely that no one has discovered it.\"

\"My hypothesis is that governments or surviving **Sovereign Defense
Directorate** organizations have deliberately suppressed this
information.\"

Kael frowned.

\"To prevent people from slaughtering mutated animals recklessly?\"

\"Yes.\"

\"If that information became public, countless survivors would attempt
to hunt creatures far stronger than themselves.\"

\"The resulting casualties would likely exceed any potential benefits.\"

Kael slowly nodded.

\"That\... does sound like something they\'d do.\"

Another brief silence followed.

\"There is one more important update.\"

\"The current date is **September 15, 2120.**\"

\"The time is **12:15**.\"

Kael blinked.

\"\...What?\"

\"You lost consciousness approximately two hours ago.\"

\"But according to my internal records\...\"

\"\...those two hours do not exist.\"

\"My system contains no record of them.\"

\"It was not merely your senses that were affected.\"

\"My processes also ceased completely.\"

\"I cannot determine whether time itself was interrupted or whether an
external force interfered with both of our perceptions.\"

\"It is beyond my current ability to explain.\"

Kael fell silent.

After another moment, Iris continued.

\"I also confirmed our current location.\"

\"We are in **Mount Myōken, Hyōgo Prefecture**. Approximately 34.929241°
North, 135.466990° East.\"

"Our current location is said to be a restricted space for the Sovereign
Nation to monitor and control flora and fauna. Due to this reason and
the mutation spreading, this location has a high range of mutated
animals with wild features."

Kael instinctively looked around the unfamiliar forest.

\"So\... we\'re surrounded in a place with danger surrounding us in all
directions.\"

\"Correct.\"

\"There is one **Joint Defense Base** located within each prefecture.\"

\"The **Joint Defense Bases** in **Nara**, **Wakayama**, **Mie**, and
**Osaka** have all been confirmed destroyed.\"

She paused briefly before continuing.

\"The nearest confirmed **Joint Defense Base** is the **Kyoto Black
Citadel**, located in **Kyoto** Prefecture.\"

\"In addition, **Kyoto** still houses one of Japan\'s **eight Regional
Command Headquarters**.\"

\"It is approximately ninety-eight kilometers from our current
location.\"

\"Under normal circumstances, that distance could be covered quickly
using a **Tier-3** or **Tier-4** **Aero-Strider**, or an
**Aero-Cruiser**.\"

\"However, because of the current location and with no availability of
any vehicles, the safest and most realistic option is to proceed on
foot. \"

Kael remained silent for several moments before letting out a slow
breath.

\"So\...I have to survive this forest\...\...and somehow make it all the
way to the **Kyoto Black Citadel** on foot.\"

Iris answered without hesitation.

\"We have no other choice unless we find a running vehicle after we
leave this forest, Kael.\"
