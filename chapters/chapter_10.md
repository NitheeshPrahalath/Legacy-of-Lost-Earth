# **Chapter 10**

**Automated Retail Store, Osaka Ruins \| September 11, 2120, 10:30**

Hearing Kael's voice, Maya gently brought her daughter Emily closer.

The food Kael had prepared was nothing more than simple roasted raccoon
meat---no seasoning, no ingredients beyond fire and time.\
Yet the aroma alone was enough to make everyone's stomach tighten with
hunger.

Cherry was the first to react.

The moment the scent reached her nose, she dashed forward, tail flicking
excitedly.

When the others arrived, they saw Kael carefully slicing the roasted
meat into thin portions and placing them on large leaves he had gathered
earlier. He divided the meat evenly, laying out seven leaf-plates.

Before anyone could react, Cherry lunged at one of the leaves and began
devouring the meat eagerly.

Kael smiled faintly, took one leaf for himself, and turned to the
group.\
"Go ahead. One each. There's no need to be afraid---this won't harm
you."

The others hesitated.

They watched Cherry chew happily, then glanced at Kael, then back at the
meat. Awkward uncertainty hung in the air.

Finally, Tanaka stepped forward.

He picked up one leaf and paused, watching Kael eat calmly. After a
brief moment, he nodded to himself and took his first bite.

That was enough.

One by one, the others followed---Maya, Nakamura, Hirota---each taking a
leaf and imitating Kael and Tanaka.

The moment the meat touched their tongues, their reasoning collapsed.

The exterior was lightly crisp, the inside juicy and tender, infused
with subtle smokiness. It was unlike anything they had ever
eaten---nothing like nutrient pods or synthetic meals produced by
automated systems.

The texture, the warmth, the richness---it awakened something forgotten.

For a moment, they completely forgot *why* they were eating it.

Compared to this, nutrient pods were painfully bland.

Expressions of pure astonishment spread across their faces.\
Soon, nothing remained of the roasted raccoon.

Hirota wiped his mouth, staring at Kael in disbelief.\
"Kael... how did you even make this taste like that? I've never eaten
anything like it. Nutrient pods don't even come close."

Maya nodded instinctively.\
"So this is... real food?"

Even Nakamura and Tanaka voiced their praise.

Kael scratched his cheek slightly, feeling a bit embarrassed.\
"I only know a little. My parents researched ancient technology and
culture. I picked up some basic cooking methods from their work."

Just as the last bite was swallowed, Iris's voice echoed in Kael's mind.

"Kael, I've noticed something unusual during my scan."

Kael mentally replied, *"What is it?"*

"According to my analysis, everyone here must have undergone some level
of mutation during the disaster---otherwise, survival would have been
unlikely. Initially, I assumed the mutation rate was uniform among
survivors. That assumption appears to be incorrect."

Kael's focus sharpened.

"Your initial strength increased by nearly forty percent after the
apocalypse," Iris continued.\
"However, the others show only minimal improvement. This suggests their
initial mutation level was significantly lower---low enough that they
did not consciously sense the change."

She refined her analysis.

"Based on my estimates, their increase should be around thirty percent.
However, they never relied on their physical strength. Instead, they
depended almost entirely on weapons such as pulse guns and stun batons.
Because of this, they were unable to perceive their enhanced strength."

She paused briefly before continuing.

"Their combat exposure was limited. Additionally, Tanaka mentioned that
his body still feels weak. This supports my conclusion that mutation
effects are less pronounced in elderly individuals. Aging bodies respond
less efficiently, suppressing visible enhancement."

Kael felt his breath catch.

"From this, I've deduced something else," Iris added.

"The mutation rate appears to be lower the farther one is from the
disaster's epicenter. The closer to the source, the stronger the
mutation."

Kael was stunned.

If that were true...\
Then regions farther away might be safer.\
Places untouched.\
Normal.

Hope flickered in his chest.

But Iris shattered it immediately.

"Kael, do not indulge that assumption. The mutation is spreading
continuously. No place is truly safe."

Her tone sharpened.

"We should move immediately. Cross the bridge while it is still
daylight. It is currently **September 11, 2120, 10:58 a.m.** High
visibility will be crucial for detecting mutated animals or other
threats."

Kael nodded inwardly, then looked at the group and spoke solemnly.\
"Everyone, now that we've finished eating, the effects of the mutated
meat should already be taking place."

The group instinctively checked themselves---clenching fists, rolling
shoulders, testing their balance.

But nothing felt different.

Nakamura frowned slightly. Hirota flexed his fingers, then shook his
head. Maya looked confused, clearly sensing no change at all.

Only Tanaka reacted.

"Yes... I can definitely feel something," he said in surprise.

Everyone turned to him.

"The change isn't dramatic," Tanaka continued thoughtfully, "but I can
feel my body slowly regaining strength. My joints feel lighter, my
breathing steadier. I would say... perhaps a **ten to fifteen percent
improvement**."

Nakamura and Hirota exchanged looks, disbelief evident on their faces.
Neither of them felt anything close to that.

Iris spoke into Kael's mind, and Kael relayed her words aloud.

"It appears Tanaka is more perceptive of changes in his body than the
others. Because his physical condition was already weakened due to age,
even a small improvement feels significant. The rest of you have also
improved---but the increase is subtle. Continuous consumption of mutated
meat will result in clearer, more noticeable enhancement."

Understanding slowly dawned on them.

Kael then straightened and continued, his tone firm.\
"We need to move out and reach the Yodo River as soon as possible.
Crossing while there's daylight is safer. It's much easier to identify
mutated animals now than it will be at night."

Nakamura nodded in agreement.\
"Yes. From my observations, the fire is still spreading. If this
continues, it could consume everything in its path."

Tanaka looked around at the group and spoke decisively.\
"If everyone agrees, we'll depart in **thirty minutes**. That should be
enough time to prepare."

No one objected. They all nodded and began gathering their belongings.

Cherry and Emily, oblivious to the tension, were still playing together
in the corner---soft laughter and gentle purring filling the room.

By **11:33 a.m.**, the group left the automated retail store and began
their journey toward the Yodo River.

Kael and Nakamura took the lead---Kael gripping his makeshift spear,
Nakamura holding his electric baton at the ready.\
At the rear, Maya covered them with the pulse gun while Tanaka held a
solid metal rod, his eyes sharp despite his age.\
Hirota walked in the center alongside Emily, who cradled Cherry in her
arms, forming a tight protective formation.

They moved cautiously, every step measured.

Iris's voice echoed in Kael's mind.\
"Kael, approximately two kilometers ahead there are several smaller
bridges. We can use them to reach a connecting landmass. From there,
multiple crossings over the Yodo River become accessible. However,
exercise caution---water sources attract a high concentration of mutated
creatures."

Kael quietly relayed the information to the group, and they adjusted
their pace.

Twenty minutes into the march, Iris's tone sharpened abruptly.\
"Kael---danger. I'm detecting a mutated monkey nearby."

Before Kael could respond, a sharp rustle came from above.

"Stay alert!" Kael warned.

The next instant, a shadow dropped from the trees.

A mutated monkey launched itself at them with explosive force.

Kael reacted immediately, thrusting his spear upward to intercept it.
The impact was brutal---the sheer momentum of the creature forced him
several steps back, his boots scraping against the ground.

Nakamura moved in without hesitation.

Crack!

The electric baton slammed into the monkey's side, electricity surging
through its body and sending it skidding away.

But the creature recovered instantly.

With a shrill screech, it sprang forward again.

Kael drove his spear in a straight thrust, the weapon slicing through
the air with a sharp whistle---but the monkey twisted mid-leap, narrowly
avoiding the strike.

Kael adjusted in an instant, turning the failed thrust into a wide
horizontal slash.

The blade tore across the monkey's right chest, drawing blood but
failing to stop it.

The mutated monkey retaliated viciously, its claws flashing as it
pressed Kael back. He barely managed to block the flurry of strikes, his
arms trembling under the force.

Then---an opening.

Kael planted his feet and surged forward, channeling all his strength
into a focused thrust.

The spear punched into the monkey's abdomen, piercing deep and driving
the breath from its lungs.

Before it could recover, Nakamura struck again---this time smashing the
electric baton into the back of its head.

The creature staggered.

Kael didn't hesitate.

With a final, decisive motion, he drove the spear straight into its
face.

The body collapsed, lifeless.

Silence followed.

The entire exchange had taken less than two minutes.

Maya and Hirota never wavered, shielding Emily the entire time. Tanaka
stood poised to intervene, his grip tight on the metal rod---ready,
should the situation have turned worse.

After the fight, Maya hurried forward, her eyes moving between Kael and
Nakamura as they steadied their breathing.\
"Are you both alright?" she asked, worry clear in her voice.

Kael took a few deep breaths, forcing the tightness from his chest.
"Just short of breath," he replied. "Nothing serious. We need to keep
moving---there's no time to rest."

Nakamura exhaled heavily, then straightened. "I'm fine. We can
continue."

The group resumed their journey.

For the next hour, they advanced cautiously, weaving through broken
streets and half-burnt structures. Along the way, they encountered
several mutated creatures---two dogs, a feral cat, a group of mutated
birds (four Japanese serows), and a raccoon. Each threat was dealt with
quickly, Kael taking the lead with his spear while Nakamura, Maya, and
Tanaka provided support and cover.

By the time they reached the nearest bridge leading to the connecting
landmass, exhaustion had begun to creep in.

What awaited them, however, was far worse than expected.

The bridge had completely collapsed. Twisted metal and broken concrete
jutted into the river like shattered bones. Worse still, the surrounding
area had become a gathering point---more than ten, perhaps fifteen
mutated animals crowded the riverbank, drinking from the water or
lurking in the shadows.

Kael felt a chill crawl up his spine.

Iris's voice sounded immediately in his mind.\
"Kael, this bridge is unusable. Crossing here is impossible. We need to
locate another route."

Kael relayed the situation to the others.

Hirota clenched his fists, his expression tense. "Then this bridge is
out of the question. Even if we find another intact one, it'll probably
be swarming with mutated animals---just like this place."

Maya hesitated for a moment, then spoke up. "There's another path
nearby. It's usually deserted. It leads to a smaller connecting landmass
first, and from there to the main one. It's longer, but safer. We'll
pass several bridges along the way and can check their condition."

The group exchanged looks. The plan wasn't ideal---but it was better
than charging into a nest of mutated beasts.

Kael nodded. "If everyone agrees, we'll follow Maya's route."

No one objected.

They changed direction, moving carefully through quieter streets. Even
so, danger followed them closely. Along the way, they encountered a
mutated sparrow, a hardened beetle, and a small swarm of aggressive
insects. Thanks to Iris's early warnings, they managed to avoid direct
confrontation, slipping past the threats before they were noticed.
