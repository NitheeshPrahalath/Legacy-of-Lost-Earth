# **Chapter 14**

**Abandoned Library Maintenance Path \| September 11, 2120, 20:00**

The wind filled the silence where the shrieking had been.

Then---

A soft thud landed against Kael's back.

Light. Controlled. Familiar.

Cherry.

The white cat settled itself across Kael's shoulders with unnatural
balance, its small paws resting firmly against him. Its fur swayed
lightly in the cold wind.

But its expression---

was completely different.

Its blue eyes were sharp and cold, silently staring toward the remaining
darkness beyond the bridge as if searching for more threats.

Its tiny paws were stained with blood.

Crow blood.

The group froze.

Hirota's breathing stopped for a second.

"...what?"

Maya stared blankly at Cherry.

Then slowly---

very slowly---

her eyes drifted toward the dead crows below.

Three clean slashes.

Perfect cuts.

Every corpse carried the same wounds.

Nakamura's pupils narrowed.

His compact instincts reacted before his thoughts did.

He looked at the distance between the bodies.

Then at the cables.

Then at Cherry.

"...those attacks..."

Tanaka's calm expression finally cracked.

"That cat..."

Emily looked at Cherry with wide eyes.

"Cherry... did that?"

Cherry ignored them.

Its ears twitched once.

Alert. Focused.

As if killing those mutated crows had been nothing more than removing
obstacles.

Kael stayed silent.

His grip on the cable tightened slightly.

He had hidden it until now.

Not because he wanted to deceive them---

but because even he still found it hard to fully accept sometimes.

Cherry wasn't normal.

Not anymore.

Iris spoke calmly inside his mind.

"Cherry seems to have killed all eight mutated crows in less than 10
seconds."

Kael exhaled quietly.

"...yeah. I noticed."

Maya's voice came out carefully, almost cautiously.

"Kael..."

Her eyes remained fixed on Cherry.

"...what exactly is that cat?"

For a moment, only the wind answered.

Then Cherry finally moved.

The white cat slowly turned its head toward Maya.

Its blue eyes locked onto hers.

A predator's gaze.

Cold. Sharp. Dangerous.

Maya instinctively stopped breathing.

Then--- Cherry blinked once.

The coldness vanished instantly.

It rubbed its head lightly against Kael's cheek and let out a soft,
innocent meow.

"...Meow."

Silence.

Hirota looked utterly lost.

"That thing just killed eight mutated crows..."

Emily stared in disbelief.

"...and now it's acting cute?"

Even Nakamura couldn't speak immediately.

Tanaka suddenly laughed weakly from the other cable.

A tired, disbelieving laugh.

"Hah... this world truly has changed."

Kael finally spoke.

"Cherry won't hurt us."

Simple words.

But firm.

Cherry quietly remained on Kael's shoulders, its tail swaying once in
the cold wind.

The group still couldn't process what had just happened.

The mutated crows. The sudden deaths. The flashes they could barely
follow.

And Cherry--- that small white cat--- killing every single crow before
any of them could even react.

The silence became suffocating.

Even the exhaustion faded for a moment under the weight of shock.

Emily slowly tightened her grip around Maya.

Her small body was trembling.

"M-Mom..."

Maya immediately pulled her closer.

"It's okay," she whispered instinctively, though even she wasn't sure
what she was trying to reassure anymore.

Her eyes remained fixed on Cherry.

Not with hatred.

Not even fear alone.

But uncertainty.

A deep human uncertainty toward something they no longer understood.

Cherry noticed their gazes.

The white cat slowly looked around at the group.

Its sharp blue eyes lingered on each person for a second.

Then its ears lowered slightly.

For the first time since killing the crows---

it looked... uneasy.

Kael felt it immediately.

Through all the blood and violence, Cherry still understood emotions.

It understood distance.

Rejection.

Kael slowly raised one hand and rubbed the side of Cherry's neck gently.

"It's okay," he said quietly.

Cherry leaned slightly into his hand.

That small action somehow made the atmosphere even stranger.

Because monsters weren't supposed to behave like that.

Tanaka's voice suddenly cut through the silence.

"We need to move!"

His tone carried urgency now.

"The blood from those crows will attract other mutated creatures!"

That snapped everyone back to reality.

Nakamura immediately looked around the darkening surroundings.

"He's right. Staying here is suicide."

Kael nodded.

"Move. Now."

The group resumed climbing.

Every movement hurt.

Their muscles were already beyond exhaustion, and now fear had forced
them to burn even more energy.

The remaining thirty-four meters--- took them nearly twenty minutes.

By the time they reached the pillar, everyone collapsed onto the
concrete surface.

No one cared about dignity anymore.

Hirota lay flat on his back, breathing heavily.

"My arms...",Emily sat against Maya silently, her face pale.

Nakamura leaned against the pillar, eyes closed, forcing his breathing
under control.

Kael sat down heavily while Cherry remained quietly beside him.

No one could stop looking at it.

The images of Cherry killing those mutated crows replayed endlessly in
their minds.

After several minutes, Maya finally spoke.

Her voice was softer now.

"Kael..."

Emily clung tightly to her mother as Maya looked directly at him.

"I know Cherry hasn't harmed any of us."

Her fingers gently stroked Emily's hair unconsciously.

"And I know... from the way it acts around you... around Emily..."

Her voice shook slightly.

"But please."

She looked at Cherry again.

"What exactly is it?"

Emily slowly looked up too.

There was fear in her eyes. But also confusion.

"Cherry... is still Cherry... right?"

The question hit harder than Maya's.

Kael looked at the white cat beside him.

Cherry quietly stared back.

Its ears lowered slightly again.

Almost as if it understood every word.

"We'll talk after we cross," Tanaka interrupted firmly.

Everyone looked toward him.

The old man's expression remained serious.

"We are still hanging above a death zone with mutated blood spread
beneath us."

He looked toward the distant ruined city.

"This is not the place for long conversations."

Nakamura nodded immediately.

"He's right."

Maya hesitated--- then slowly nodded too.

"...fine."

Kael silently exhaled in relief.

Not because he wanted to hide the truth forever.

But because even he didn't fully know how to explain Cherry anymore.

Was it still just mutation?

Or something beyond that?

Iris spoke calmly inside his mind.

"Emotional instability among the group has increased significantly."

"I noticed."

"Kael, this could increase the trust level of the entire group."

Kael glanced at Cherry.

The white cat quietly climbed back onto his shoulders and rested there
silently.

"...we'll deal with that later," Kael thought.

The group resumed moving.

This time downward.

The final descent.

Compared to climbing, it was easier---

but their bodies were already breaking down.

Still, step by step---

they crossed.

When Kael's feet finally touched solid ground on the other side of the
bridge, a strange feeling hit him.

Relief.

Not safety.

Just relief that they hadn't died crossing.

Iris immediately spoke.

"We need to locate shelter. Remaining in the open significantly
increases encounter probability with hostile mutated organisms."

Kael nodded faintly.

Even standing was becoming difficult now.

The bridge crossing had drained them too deeply.

The sky has turned dark, the sun had completely disappeared, leaving the
red glow of the undying flames at the edge.

Still--- they couldn't stop.

Kael turned toward the group.

"We need a safe place to rest."

His breathing was still uneven.

"Right now we're too weak. If something stronger appears..."

He didn't finish the sentence.

He didn't need to.

Nakamura scanned the surroundings.

"Moving through open streets is risky. Especially after dark."

Tanaka suddenly spoke.

"There's a library nearby, The one I used to manage as my family
heritage."

His tired eyes carried faint familiarity now, as if remembering an older
world.

"It's around fifteen minutes from here if we take the secluded
maintenance paths."

Maya frowned slightly.

"A library?"

Nakamura replied instead of Tanaka.

"The building was reinforced during the automation era. Emergency
shutters. Backup storage sections. Thick walls."

Kael listened carefully.

"Mutated activity around the area?"

"Should be Low," Tanaka replied. "The area was mostly administrative.
Less population density."

Iris analyzed quickly.

"Probability of viable temporary shelter: high."

Kael looked toward the others.

Hirota gave a tired nod first.

"Anywhere with walls sounds good to me..."

Maya held Emily closer.

Emily looked completely drained now.

"...I just want somewhere to sleep," she whispered weakly.

Nakamura crossed his arms briefly.

"It's the best option we have."

The group started walking.

Every single step reminded them how badly the bridge crossing had
damaged their bodies.

Their arms still trembled from hanging onto the cables for so long.
Their palms were torn raw. Even breathing felt heavier now.

Tanaka guided them through secluded side paths away from the main roads.

The streets were narrow and overgrown.

Collapsed vehicles blocked parts of the route. Burned buildings stood
like hollow corpses beneath the darkening sky.

The distant glow of firestorms still painted parts of the horizon red.

Thankfully---

mutated activity remained low.

Even so, danger still found them.

A mutated deer emerged first from between abandoned cars.

Its body was unnaturally thin yet oversized, patches of darkened flesh
stretched tightly over distorted muscles. One antler had twisted into a
jagged spiral.

The creature charged the moment it noticed movement.

Nakamura stepped forward instantly despite his exhaustion.

"I'll take it."

Kael moved beside him anyway.

"Don't overdo it."

The fight lasted less than thirty seconds.

Nakamura struck first with his electric baton, disrupting the creature's
charge long enough for Kael's spear to pierce through its neck.

Even after killing it, both men remained still for a moment, breathing
heavily.

The fight itself wasn't difficult.

Their exhaustion was.

Later, two mutated raccoons attacked from the side alleys.

Their claws scraped sparks off the concrete walls as they lunged.

Cherry reacted before anyone else.

A white blur flashed forward.

One raccoon slammed into a wall lifelessly before the others even
realized what happened.

The second froze instinctively---

just long enough for Kael to kill it with a downward thrust.

Nobody commented about Cherry this time.

But everyone noticed.

Less than twenty minutes later--- they finally reached the library.

The moment the building came into view, Tanaka slowed slightly.

For a second, something nostalgic crossed his exhausted face.

"...we made it."

The library stood intact among the ruined surroundings.

But that wasn't what caught their attention.

"This..."

Nakamura stopped walking completely.

"This... how are there so many trees here?"

His voice carried genuine shock.

Everyone looked around.

The library was surrounded by massive trees and thick shrubs on nearly
all sides. Dense vegetation covered the area so heavily that it almost
looked like a forest had swallowed the building whole.

Only the backside remained relatively clear, where several small-scale
textile manufacturing buildings still stood abandoned.

Tanaka stared blankly. "How did these trees even grow here...?"

Kael looked between them. "What's wrong?"

Nakamura pointed toward the towering trees.

"This place used to be empty."

"Completely empty," Tanaka added immediately. "There were only patches
of grass around the library."

Emily slowly looked upward. "These trees are huge..."

The branches stretched unnaturally high, their trunks thicker than
normal trees should have been after such a short period.

Iris spoke inside Kael's mind. "Environmental mutation influence
detected."

Kael listened carefully.

"Mutation levels within the atmosphere appear to be affecting plant life
as well." Her tone remained analytical.

"Observed effects currently include accelerated growth and increased
life force signatures. No immediate hostile traits detected."

Kael frowned slightly.

"So even plants are mutating now..."

"It is highly probable."

Kael looked toward the group.

"It seems the mutation phenomenon is affecting all living organisms."

His eyes briefly scanned the trees.

"Just stay careful around them. If there's no immediate danger, we
proceed inside."

Everyone agreed silently.

Even with their exhaustion, Nakamura and Kael still checked the
surrounding area first.

Nakamura inspected the nearby alleys while Kael scanned the tree lines
carefully.

Cherry remained unusually alert throughout.

After several minutes, Iris completed a wider scan.

The result surprised even Kael.

"No hostile biological signatures detected within a one-kilometer
radius."

Kael blinked slightly.

"None?"

"Correct."

That was abnormal.

In this world---

silence itself felt unnatural now.

Nakamura returned shortly after.

"Clear."

Kael nodded slowly.

"Then we settle here for tonight."

They approached the main entrance.

The reinforced library doors remained locked.

Tanaka stepped forward first, placing his trembling hand against the
biometric scanner.

The system flickered weakly.

For a moment, everyone thought the power was dead.

Then---

Access Granted.

The heavy locks disengaged with a mechanical click.

A faint sigh escaped Tanaka.

"...still working."

The group entered quickly.

The moment the doors shut behind them--- everyone collapsed.

Nobody cared about appearances anymore.

Maya sat down heavily against a wall while holding Emily close.

Hirota practically dropped onto the floor.

Nakamura leaned silently against a bookshelf, exhausted beyond words.

Kael lowered himself slowly near the entrance while Cherry curled
quietly beside him.

The strain from the journey hit all at once now that they had finally
stopped moving.

Silence filled the library.

Only heavy breathing remained.

Then Maya spoke immediately.

"Kael."

Her voice was serious this time.

"Please tell us about Cherry."

Her eyes moved toward the white cat resting beside him.

"We need to know what she really is."

The room grew silent again.

Everyone's attention shifted toward Kael.

Even Hirota slowly sat up despite his exhaustion.

Cherry quietly lifted its head.

Its blue eyes moved toward Kael.

Almost as if waiting.

Iris spoke calmly inside his mind.

"Kael. It is advisable to explain Cherry's situation."

A brief pause.

"Clarification is necessary to maintain trust within the group."

Kael remained silent for several seconds.

Then he took a deep breath.

"As I told you before, I found Cherry inside an abandoned food
distribution hub."

He gently stroked Cherry's fur as he spoke.

"She was trapped between collapsed walls, so I rescued her."

His eyes lowered slightly.

"But during an accident... she drank my blood."

The room stayed silent.

"After that, Cherry started mutating."

Kael looked at the group calmly.

"But unlike the other mutated animals, she never showed any aggression
toward me."

Cherry quietly rubbed its head against Kael's hand.

Kael gave a faint smile.

"While she may look small... her speed and strength have already
surpassed mine."

His hand rested gently on Cherry's head.

"But no matter what changes..."

Cherry let out a soft purr.

"She'll always remain as Cherry."
