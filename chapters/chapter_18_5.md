# **Chapter 18.5**

## World Data --- IRIS Archive Playback

**Kael** opened the archive as he sat in the middle of the forest.

**Iris** showed him fragments of the world as it stood **one month
before humanity\'s collapse**, cached and dormant until now.

## I. Sovereign Defense Directorate

Before the collapse, the old global order had fractured into competing
**Sovereign Nations**. Each maintained its own **Sovereign Defense
Directorate**, organized into **five military branches**.

### **Military Branches**

-   **Territorial Pacification Vanguard** --- Mechanized infantry,
    armored convoys, territorial defense, and border security.

-   **Hydro-Resource Armada** --- Naval operations, ocean security, and
    protection of the massive **desalinization fleets** that supplied
    coastal cities.

-   **Atmospheric Supremacy Command** --- Air superiority, aerial
    defense, and atmospheric operations.

-   **Orbital Strike & Logistics Division** --- Satellites, orbital
    infrastructure, communications, and lunar operations.

-   **Verdant Guard** --- A classified branch responsible for covert
    missions, intelligence, and threats the government never
    acknowledged publicly.

### **Officer Rank Structure**

-   **Sub-Prime**

-   **Captain-Prime**

-   **Major-Tactical**

-   **Commander-Regent**

-   **Colonel-Logis**

-   *(Higher ranks existed beyond these.)*

**Nakamura\'s rank --- Commander-Regent ---** placed him at
**battalion-level command**, a position earned through service rather
than appointment.

### **Military Organization**

-   **47 Joint Bases**

-   Reported to **8 Regional Commands**

-   All ultimately answered to the **Supreme Defense Command** located
    in **Nagano**.

Three of those forty-seven bases now meant something to Kael:

-   **Izumo Coastal Base** --- Former station of **Nakamura**, serving
    under the **Hydro-Resource Armada**.

-   **Tenri Security Base (Nara)** --- **Verdant Guard** installation.
    **Confirmed destroyed.**

-   **Hikone Defense Base (Shiga)** --- **Territorial Pacification
    Vanguard** stronghold approximately **120 kilometers away**.

## II. Societal Caste Hierarchy

Long before the collapse, humanity had already stopped organizing itself
by wealth alone. What mattered was **biological reality**---how real,
how natural, how *human* your daily life was still permitted to be.

> **Class 4 --- The Foundation** *(≈95% of the population)*
> Standardized, synthetic, comfortable. Guaranteed housing, work, and
> food---entirely lab-made. Kael\'s world, before the Awakening.
>
> **Class 3 --- The Enterprise Sector** *(≈4.9%)* Industrialists and
> corporate managers. Premium synthetic food, engineered to look and
> taste real. Immense wealth, no real political power.
>
> **Class 2 --- The Directorate** *(≈0.1%)* Judges, governors, generals,
> intelligence directors. Split internally: lower officials remained on
> synthetic rations, while high-level officials alone were granted
> access to the **Biosphere Rations**---real, soil-grown food.
>
> **Class 1 --- The Sovereign Elite** *(≈0.01%)* Global chancellors,
> hereditary megacorp owners, top military command. Lived within sealed
> **Eden Enclaves**, with fully organic diets and simulated natural
> environments.

Every privilege the archive had shown so far---flight altitude, medical
access, food---traced back to this same ladder.

## III. Sovereign Education Architecture

Every citizen passed through the same **Foundation Academies**, grades 1
through 12, ending in a **Global Metric Baseline (GMB)** score out of
1,500. That score decided which of four college tiers a person entered.

-   **Tier 1 (Apex)** --- 1300--1500 points. Macro/global-scale
    training.

-   **Tier 2 (High)** --- 1000--1299 points. Regional/corporate-scale
    training.

-   **Tier 3 (Mid)** --- 600--999 points. Community/local-scale
    training.

-   **Tier 4 (Common)** --- Under 600 points. Domestic/routine-scale
    training.

Every tier taught the same eight foundational disciplines---only the
depth, scale, and technology available differed. Among them:
**Techno-Symbiosis & Compute Mechanics**, the discipline governing
programming, AI coordination, and hardware systems.

**Kael** and **Hirota** had both scored into **Tier 2**, enrolled at
**Osaka Metaverse College**---one of many colleges offering the standard
curriculum, but among the highest-scoring institutions permitted to
teach it at regional scale. Both had specialized in the **Cybernetics
Department**, within Techno-Symbiosis & Compute Mechanics: at Tier 2,
this meant advanced prompt architecture, full-stack cyber-defense
orchestration, and forensic auditing of corrupted or biased code.
Neither of them had known, a month ago, how little of it would matter
now.

## IV. Transit Systems

Ground vehicles no longer used wheels.

Every civilian and military vehicle operated through **magnetic
levitation**, using **repulsor systems** instead of tires and powered by
either **hydrogen cells** or **fusion cores**.

### **Vehicle Classes**

#### **Aero-Striders**

Single-rider flying vehicles.

Models ranged from:

-   **Drifter** --- Disposable low-altitude commuter available to
    ordinary citizens.

-   Higher-tier corporate and government models with progressively
    greater altitude and speed privileges.

#### **Aero-Cruisers**

Multi-passenger aircraft used for:

-   Public transportation

-   Freight transport

-   Armored civilian carriers

-   Military troop deployment

**Flight altitude itself was a privilege.**

The higher a citizen\'s social status, the higher they were legally
permitted to fly.

Now, none of it functioned.

Without the power grid or maintenance infrastructure, every vehicle
remaining in the ruins had become **dead weight**.

## V. Armament

Traditional **chemical-propellant firearms** had disappeared decades
before the Collapse.

They had been replaced by **energy weapons**, manufactured from **graded
energy-core materials**.

### **Known Weapon Types**

> **Pulse Guns** *(Maya\'s weapon)* Standard civilian and
> retired-personnel firearm. Reliable, but dependent on limited energy
> cells.
>
> **Electric Batons** *(Nakamura\'s weapon)* Standard close-quarters
> weapon for security personnel.
>
> **Directorate-Grade Weapons** Reserved exclusively for active military
> personnel and vastly superior to civilian equipment.

### **Kael\'s Weapon**

Kael\'s spear, forged from **scavenged unrefined alloy**, represented
the **lowest technological weapon tier**.

Anything stronger required advanced materials that no surviving human
had yet recovered.

## VI. Medical Systems

Civilian emergency medicine centered around compact **Field Medical
Kits**, like the one carried by **Maya**.

Capabilities included:

-   **Diagnostic scanning**

-   **Guided treatment protocols**

-   **Adaptive medical bandages** capable of sealing wounds immediately
    while administering treatment

This represented the highest level of healthcare available to ordinary
citizens.

True **Regenerative Medicine**---technology capable of rebuilding
damaged tissue instead of merely stabilizing injuries---had always been
restricted to the highest levels of **government** and **corporate
authority**.

## VII. Food Classification

For over **fifty years**, the overwhelming majority of humanity survived
entirely on **synthetic food**.

Common examples included:

-   **Nutrient Pods**

-   **Protein Slabs**

-   **Automated Synthetic Meals**

These were engineered to imitate flavors that most living citizens had
never experienced naturally.

### **Real Food**

Authentic food still existed before the Collapse, but it was tightly
controlled.

Distribution followed the same hierarchy governing:

-   **Flight privileges**

-   **Medical access**

-   **Government authority**

The higher one\'s social standing, the greater their access to genuine
food.

At the highest levels of power, real food became almost exclusive.

### **Unknown Classification**

The meat Kael had eaten in the ruins fit neither known category.

It was:

-   **Not synthetic**

-   **Not verified as safe by IRIS**

-   **Unlike any food recorded before the Collapse**

It belonged to something entirely new---something the old world\'s
classification systems had never anticipated.

**End of Archive**

The light faded from **Kael\'s wrist**.
