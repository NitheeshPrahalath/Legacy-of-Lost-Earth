**DEPARTMENT OF STRATEGIC FORESIGHT AND GLOBAL INFRASTRUCTURE**
**CLASSIFIED DOSSIER — AERO-CRUISER FLEET CATALOG**
**YEAR:** 2120 | **CLEARANCE:** LEVEL OMEGA

**SUBJECT:** Multi-Point Mag-Lev Vehicles (Aero-Cruisers)

**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:**
The "Aero-Cruiser" classification encompasses all multi-point magnetic levitation vehicles designed to transport multiple passengers or heavy cargo. The era of vulcanized rubber tires and asphalt friction is dead. All models rely on quad (or hex) ground-effect repulsors. Like the Aero-Striders (bikes), altitude and speed are strictly gatekept by social caste and military clearance.

---

## 1. The Prole-Pod (Class 4 / Mass Transit)

* **Model Name:** V-Tek "Hab-Crawler" Micro-Van
* **Purpose:** Hyper-cheap carpooling for low-tier synthetic factory workers.
* **Description:** A featureless, gray, rectangular box that hovers barely 0.2 meters off the ground. No windows, only internal AR screens playing state-approved media. Seats 6 uncomfortably.
* **Energy Required:** Recycled Algal-Battery Bank.
* **Top Speed:** 60 km/h (Normal) / 85 km/h (Modified, risks battery fire).
* **Cost:** 12,000 Global Credits (Often leased collectively by hab-blocks).
* **Gov Controlled:** Automated driving only; routes are pre-programmed by the state grid.
* **Illegal Mods:** Slicing the passenger weight sensors to cram 10-12 people inside for cheaper transit.

## 2. The Standard Civilian (Class 4/3 / Daily Driver)

* **Model Name:** Torrent "Nomad" 4-Door
* **Purpose:** The standard family sedan of 2120.
* **Description:** Reliable, blocky, and safe. Features transparent aluminum windows and a manual-override yoke for emergency steering. Used by mid-level academy graduates.
* **Energy Required:** Dual Solid-State Hydrogen Cells.
* **Top Speed:** 150 km/h (Normal) / 200 km/h (Modified).
* **Cost:** 25,000 Global Credits.
* **Gov Controlled:** Speed governed by structural firmware. Mandatory internal cabin audio recording.
* **Illegal Mods:** "Sensor Blinders"—small scrambling chips that temporarily blind traffic-enforcement drones to avoid toll taxes in corporate zones.

## 3. The Heavy Freighter (Logistics / Class 3)

* **Model Name:** Atlas "Behemoth" Cargo-Lifter
* **Purpose:** Moving massive quantities of synthetic food paste, building materials, and raw hydrogen.
* **Description:** A massive, 18-meter long hex-levitation truck. The cab is separated from the cargo by a reinforced bulkhead. Drives almost entirely autonomously on dedicated freight mag-lanes.
* **Energy Required:** Industrial Hydrogen-Plasma Core.
* **Top Speed:** 110 km/h (Normal) / 140 km/h (Modified).
* **Cost:** 150,000 Global Credits.
* **Gov Controlled:** Cargo bays have integrated chemical sniffers to detect organic contraband.
* **Illegal Mods:** Smugglers build secret compartments inside the massive thermal cooling baffles to hide untaxed goods or illegal refugees.

## 4. The Grid-Bus (Public Transit)

* **Model Name:** Omni-Transit "Leviathan"
* **Purpose:** High-capacity urban transit.
* **Description:** A heavily armored, articulated bus. Built to withstand Class 4 sector riots, it features electrified outer hull plating to prevent citizens from hanging onto the exterior.
* **Energy Required:** Grid-Tethered (Draws power directly from inductive coils built into the city streets).
* **Top Speed:** 90 km/h.
* **Cost:** 200,000 Global Credits (State funded).
* **Gov Controlled:** Total state control.
* **Illegal Mods:** Criminal syndicates often tap into the Leviathan's grid-tether frequency, siphoning free city power to run underground hydroponic weed labs.

## 5. The Corporate Executive (Class 3 / Luxury)

* **Model Name:** Ominova "Regalia"
* **Purpose:** Chauffeuring mid-to-high corporate managers above the smog.
* **Description:** Sleek, completely silent, and heavily insulated. The cabin features built-in air purifiers that scrub the toxic urban air into pine-scented breezes. Hovers at a comfortable 30 meters.
* **Energy Required:** Micro-Fission Cell.
* **Top Speed:** 350 km/h (Normal) / 420 km/h (Modified).
* **Cost:** 250,000 Global Credits.
* **Gov Controlled:** GPS monitored, but generally ignored by local police due to corporate privilege.
* **Illegal Mods:** Installing unregistered "Aggressive" AI driving profiles that illegally force lower-class vehicles out of their lane.

## 6. The Bio-Vault (Verdant Guard / Agri-Corp)

* **Model Name:** Agri-Sec "Ceres" Armored Transport
* **Purpose:** Transporting real, organic food, soil, and seeds to the Class 1 and Class 2 elites.
* **Description:** Looks like a rolling bank vault. Heavily armored, climate-controlled, and guarded by automated internal turrets. The interior is kept at a perfect 4°C to preserve organic matter.
* **Energy Required:** Commercial Helium-3 Reactor.
* **Top Speed:** 250 km/h.
* **Cost:** 800,000 Global Credits.
* **Gov Controlled:** Only the Verdant Guard and licensed Tier 1 Agri-Corps can operate them.
* **Illegal Mods:** Attempting to slice the biometric locks on the cargo bay is punishable by immediate orbital kinetic strike.

## 7. The Enforcer (Law Enforcement / TPV)

* **Model Name:** Aegis "Bastion" Interceptor
* **Purpose:** Police cruiser designed for high-speed pursuit and barricade breaching.
* **Description:** Brutalist design, painted high-visibility white and blue. Equipped with a frontal kinetic-ramming shield, EMP harpoons, and deployable tire-shredding nanite spikes (for disabling older, wheel-based tech).
* **Energy Required:** Dual Hydrogen Core with kinetic recovery.
* **Top Speed:** 480 km/h.
* **Cost:** 300,000 Global Credits.
* **Gov Controlled:** Directly linked to the A.E.D.U. surveillance grid.
* **Illegal Mods (Corrupt Cops):** Disabling the automated weapons-discharge log to use excessive force without central command oversight.

## 8. The Syndicate Racer (Underground / Black Market)

* **Model Name:** Vektor "Wraith" GTR
* **Purpose:** Illegal street racing and high-speed courier runs for the black market.
* **Description:** A chopped, widened, and dangerously unstable chassis. The interior is gutted of all safety features to reduce weight. It runs so hot that the exterior paint frequently blisters and peels off during a race.
* **Energy Required:** Unstable Liquid Hydrogen / Plasma Dump.
* **Top Speed:** 650 km/h (Normal tuned) / 880 km/h (Modified with plasma injection).
* **Cost:** ~600,000 Global Credits (Parts and black-market labor).
* **Gov Controlled:** Highly illegal. Possession of a Wraith chassis carries a 20-year sentence in a penal labor camp.
* **Illegal Mods:** Active aerodynamic fins made from stolen military smart-alloy, and rear-facing oil/slick dispensers.

## 9. The Sovereign Apex (Class 1 / World Leaders)

* **Model Name:** Aurelia "Pantheon" Flight-Yacht
* **Purpose:** Transporting the absolute global elite (Class 1) between Eden Enclaves and command centers.
* **Description:** It does not drive; it flies. A massive, teardrop-shaped vessel coated in radar-absorbent metamaterials. The interior is a palatial suite featuring real wood, silk, and a fully organic wet-bar. It utilizes an Aero-Plasma Deflector Cone to break the sound barrier without creating a sonic boom over the city.
* **Energy Required:** Military-grade Helium-3 Fusion Core.
* **Top Speed:** Mach 1.1 (1,350 km/h).
* **Cost:** 12 Million Global Credits.
* **Gov Controlled:** It *is* the government.
* **Illegal Mods:** None. Modifying a Pantheon is considered treason.

## 10. The Main Battle Cruiser (Military / TPV)

* **Model Name:** TPV "Juggernaut" Strike-Cruiser
* **Purpose:** Heavy mechanized infantry transport and frontline border assault.
* **Description:** A hovering fortress. It carries 12 heavily armed Conscript-Sentinels and is equipped with a roof-mounted twin-plasma cannon. It uses localized gravity-shearing fields to crush obstacles (or enemy vehicles) beneath it as it hovers.
* **Energy Required:** Military Micro-Fusion Core.
* **Top Speed:** Mach 1.4 (1,700 km/h). *Note: At this speed, it deploys a forward kinetic-shield to survive atmospheric friction, turning it into a supersonic battering ram.*
* **Cost:** 850 Million Global Credits.
* **Gov Controlled:** Absolute military asset.
* **Illegal Mods:** None. All weapons and steering yokes are DNA-locked to authorized Sub-Primes or higher.
