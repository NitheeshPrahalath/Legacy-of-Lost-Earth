**DEPARTMENT OF STRATEGIC FORESIGHT AND GLOBAL INFRASTRUCTURE**
**CLASSIFIED DOSSIER — AERO-STRIDER CIVILIAN & LAW ENFORCEMENT PLATFORMS**
**YEAR:** 2120 | **CLEARANCE:** LEVEL OMEGA

**SUBJECT:** Multi-Tier Urban Mag-Lev Bike (Aero-Strider) Fleet

**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:**
The civilian transit ecosystem is strictly stratified. Movement is a privilege, regulated by the state and limited by the hardware a citizen is permitted to purchase. The following six models represent the complete spectrum of non-military Aero-Striders available in 2120, spanning from the disposable working-class commuter to the ultra-elite luxury craft, plus the dedicated state law enforcement interceptor.

---

## 1. The Scrapper (Class 4 / Underclass)

* **Model Name:** V-Tek "Drifter" Mk. II
* **Classification:** Ultra-Low Altitude Pedestrian Commuter
* **Purpose:** To get the lowest-tier factory workers from their mega-block to their work sector.
* **Appearance:** Looks like a battered, motorized scooter without wheels. Built from unpainted stamped aluminum and salvaged plastics. Wires are often exposed.
* **Technology/Power Source:** Cheap, single-use Algal-Battery cores. They cannot be recharged; they must be recycled at state kiosks for a minor credit refund.
* **Performance:**
* *Normal Top Speed:* 45 km/h.
* *Modified Top Speed:* 75 km/h (engine prone to catching fire).
* *Hover Altitude:* Hard-locked at 0.3 meters (barely clears a curb).


* **Cost:** 800 Global Credits (Highly affordable, often subsidized by employers).
* **Who Can Own It:** Any citizen with a pulse and a Class-E transit pass.
* **Illegal Modifications:** "Bypassing the limiter" is common, giving it slightly more speed but destroying the battery life. Criminals often hollow out the battery casing to smuggle synthetic stimulants.
* **Government Control:** All Drifters have a mandatory state kill-switch. If a riot breaks out, the local grid sends a pulse that drops every Drifter in a 5-mile radius to the ground.

## 2. The Workhorse (Class 4 / Logistics)

* **Model Name:** Torrent-8 Utility Strider *(Previously Analyzed)*
* **Classification:** Low-Altitude Commuter & Cargo Strider
* **Purpose:** Civilian transportation, urban commuting, and light courier logistics.
* **Appearance:** A rigid, blocky, highly durable frame made of recycled carbon-composite plastics. Features a scuffed matte-industrial paint job and heavy-duty electromagnetic cargo plates on the rear.
* **Technology/Power Source:** Solid-State Hydrogen Cells (swappable at commercial stations).
* **Performance:**
* *Normal Top Speed:* 180 km/h (Structurally Governed).
* *Modified Top Speed:* 250 km/h.
* *Hover Altitude:* Locked at 1 meter.


* **Cost:** 8,500 Global Credits.
* **Who Can Own It:** Registered citizens with Class-C Transit passes (usually couriers, mid-level technicians, and licensed wasteland scavengers).
* **Illegal Modifications:**
* *Spoof-Cap:* Tricking the firmware into allowing the bike to hover up to 15 meters to clear security fences.
* *Hydrogen Overclocking:* Rerouting safety regulators for a 40% speed boost, risking catastrophic engine explosion.


* **Government Control:** Mandatory firmware updates track all GPS locations. Missing a software update permanently grounds the vehicle.

## 3. The Executive (Class 3 / Corporate)

* **Model Name:** Ominova "Sylph" 900
* **Classification:** Mid-Altitude Corporate Transport
* **Purpose:** Fast, comfortable commuting for mid-level corporate managers (Tier 3 and Tier 4 academy graduates).
* **Appearance:** Sleek, aerodynamic, and completely enclosed. It resembles a teardrop of polished obsidian. It features a sealed, climate-controlled cabin for a single rider, keeping the city smog out.
* **Technology/Power Source:** High-density Micro-Fission cells (safe, but heavily regulated).
* **Performance:**
* *Normal Top Speed:* 320 km/h.
* *Modified Top Speed:* N/A (The internal AI will lock the user inside and drive to the nearest precinct if tampering is detected).
* *Hover Altitude:* Permitted up to 50 meters (allows them to bypass ground-level traffic).


* **Cost:** 85,000 Global Credits.
* **Who Can Own It:** Class 3 Citizens with Class-B Airspace passes.
* **Illegal Modifications:** Black-market "Ghost Chips" that spoof the transponder, making the Sylph look like a police drone on traffic control radar to avoid speeding fines.
* **Government Control:** Audio/Visual feeds inside the cabin are directly accessible by the state at all times.

## 4. The Aristocrat (Class 2 / High Official)

* **Model Name:** Aurelia "Zenith" V-TOL
* **Classification:** High-Altitude Luxury Cruiser
* **Purpose:** Pure status symbol and high-speed transit for the wealthy elite and government officials.
* **Appearance:** A masterpiece of engineering. Trimmed in extinct organic materials (real wood inlays, real leather seats). It operates with complete silence.
* **Technology/Power Source:** Commercial-grade Helium-3 Fusion Core (scaled down from military tech).
* **Performance:**
* *Normal Top Speed:* 600 km/h.
* *Modified Top Speed:* 850 km/h.
* *Hover Altitude:* Permitted up to 500 meters (flying above the city smog layer entirely).


* **Cost:** 1.2 Million Global Credits.
* **Who Can Own It:** Class 2 Officials and top-tier Class 3 Megacorp owners (Class-A Airspace pass required).
* **Illegal Modifications:** Installing military-grade localized EMP generators to shut down paparazzi drones or lower-class vehicles that get too close.
* **Government Control:** Virtually immune to local traffic laws, but strictly monitored by the OSL (Orbital Strike & Logistics) to ensure they do not approach military installations.

## 5. The Enforcer (Law Enforcement / Police)

* **Model Name:** Aegis "Interceptor" Mk-IV
* **Classification:** Urban Pursuit & Riot Suppression Strider
* **Purpose:** Used by the Territorial Pacification Vanguard (TPV) police units to chase down criminals, suppress riots, and enforce traffic laws.
* **Appearance:** Heavy, aggressive, and painted stark white and black with blinding red/blue strobe arrays. Features heavy ramming bumpers and reinforced side-panel armor.
* **Technology/Power Source:** Dual Solid-State Hydrogen Cells with an emergency kinetic-recovery system.
* **Performance:**
* *Normal Top Speed:* 450 km/h.
* *Hover Altitude:* Up to 100 meters.


* **Cost:** 120,000 Global Credits (State funded).
* **Who Can Own It:** Law Enforcement Only. Possession of police livery by a civilian is a capital offense.
* **Illegal Modifications (By Corrupt Cops):** Disabling the internal dash-cams and GPS trackers to conduct off-the-books raids or shakedowns in Class 4 sectors.
* **Government Control:** Features an onboard A.I. that automatically calculates the trajectory of fleeing suspects and can take control of the bike from the officer to execute precision PIT maneuvers. Includes forward-facing non-lethal sonic cannons.

## 6. The Phantom (Military / Top Tier)

* **Model Name:** Kestrel-V Apex *(Previously Analyzed)*
* **Classification:** Sub-Orbital Mag-Lev Flight Bike
* **Purpose:** Stealth Reconnaissance & High-Altitude Border Patrol.
* **Appearance:** Forged from Programmable Magneto-Rheological Smart-Alloy. Retractable aerodynamic winglets. Uses chameleon metamaterials for active optic and thermal cloaking.
* **Technology/Power Source:** Micro-Fusion Isotope Core (Helium-3 / Deuterium).
* **Performance:**
* *Normal Top Speed:* Mach 1.2 (1,470 km/h).
* *Hover Altitude:* Up to 5,000 meters (True flight).


* **Cost:** 450 Million Global Credits.
* **Who Can Own It:** Sovereign State Military tier 1 operatives.
* **Illegal Modifications:** Black-market "Neuromancers" attempt to slice the haptic helmet to allow non-military minds to interface with the steering, often resulting in fatal cerebral hemorrhaging.
* **Government Control:** Absolute. Requires DNA/neural-locked biosensors to operate.

**CLASSIFIED DOSSIER — AERO-STRIDER SPECIALIZED PLATFORMS**
**YEAR:** 2120 | **CLEARANCE:** LEVEL OMEGA

**SUBJECT:** High-Velocity Competitive / Syndicate Racing Platform

**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:**
Racing in 2120 exists in two forms: state-sanctioned corporate showcases (highly regulated, Class 3 drivers) and black-market syndicate circuits (lethal, unregulated, run through the submerged tunnels and industrial sectors of the Class 4 slums). The following model bridges the gap—a street-legal sports model that is heavily manipulated by the criminal underworld for extreme racing.

---

## The Apex Predator (Sports/Racing Class)

* **Model Name:** Vektor "Screaming Mantis" RS-9
* **Classification:** High-Performance Competition Strider
* **Purpose:** Built for corporate-sponsored circuit racing. On the street, it is the ultimate expression of civilian speed, driven by reckless trust-fund heirs and high-stakes syndicate runners.
* **Appearance:** Aggressively minimal. Stripped of all non-essential armor and utility space. It features a hunched, bio-mechanical silhouette resembling a predatory insect. The rider lies almost completely flat against the chassis. Instead of standard mag-lev discs, it features twin exposed "Quantum Ring" thrusters that burn a violent, ionized violet color.
* **Technology/Power Source:** Over-pressurized Liquid Hydrogen Cores paired with a localized kinetic-dump capacitor (which allows for brief, explosive bursts of acceleration).
* **Performance:**
* *Normal Top Speed:* 520 km/h (Factory locked for street legality).
* *Modified Top Speed (Unrestricted):* 780+ km/h.
* *Hover Altitude:* Dynamic vectoring. Can ride anywhere from 0.5 meters to 15 meters, allowing it to bank off vertical retaining walls in tight corners.


* **Cost:** 280,000 Global Credits.
* **Who Can Own It:** Wealthy Class 3 Citizens or high-tier Class 4 Syndicate bosses operating through shell corporations. Requires a specialized "Class-S High Velocity" licensing tier.

### Operational Nuance & Criminal Misuse

* **The "Dead Man's Turn":** Because the bike lacks military-grade inertia dampeners, turning at 700 km/h exerts massive G-forces on the rider. Black-market racers undergo illegal cybernetic spinal reinforcements just to survive the corners without snapping their necks.
* **Illegal Modifications:**
* *Thermal Bleed Vents:* Modders strip the exhaust baffles, allowing the engine to dump raw heat laterally. This is used offensively in syndicate races to melt the plastic chassis of pursuing opponents.
* *EMP Snare:* A highly illegal disruption coil installed in the tail. It drops a localized magnetic mine that shorts out the levitation discs of trailing riders, sending them crashing into the pavement.


* **Government Control:** The state tolerates the black-market racing circuits because they act as a "pressure valve" for the restless lower classes. However, if an RS-9 enters a restricted corporate sector, automated anti-air turrets will vaporize it instantly, as its speed profile makes it indistinguishable from a kinetic missile.
* **Maintenance Requirements:** Extreme. The liquid hydrogen cores require cryogenic storage. After a single high-stakes race, the internal magnetic bearings usually fuse from the heat and must be entirely replaced.
