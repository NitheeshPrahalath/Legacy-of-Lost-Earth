Create the **official master insignia of the fictional Sovereign Defense Directorate (SDD)**.

Use the attached reference image as the **primary visual reference for the badge's physical appearance, proportions, geometry, material construction, layering, lighting behavior, and overall visual language**.

The final result must look like the same badge design, not a reinterpretation into a different emblem.

---

## CORE FORM

Design a **perfectly symmetrical vertical diamond-shaped military insignia**.

The badge is a precision-engineered armored emblem approximately twice as tall as its central width.

The outer silhouette consists of four extremely sharp points:

* top point
* right point
* bottom point
* left point

The four points create a clean diamond silhouette.

There is **no rounded shield shape**.

There is **no circular outer ring**.

There is **no traditional heraldic border**.

There is **no decorative crest surrounding the badge**.

The overall silhouette must remain severe, geometric and architectural.

---

## FOUR PRIMARY ARMOR ASSEMBLIES

The badge is constructed around **four large interlocking metallic structures**, arranged symmetrically around the central void.

Each structure occupies one directional quadrant:

**NORTH — EAST — SOUTH — WEST**

Each major structure should resemble a thick, angular piece of precision-machined armor.

The four pieces must appear to physically converge toward the center while remaining separated by narrow recessed gaps.

Each plate contains:

* large planar metallic surfaces
* deep bevels
* sharp angular transitions
* raised structural ridges
* secondary recessed plates
* precision-machined edges
* dark separation channels
* different levels of physical depth

The geometry should feel **manufactured**, not sculpted or ornamental.

---

## CENTRAL VOID

At the exact center of the badge is a **small, sharply defined dark geometric void**.

The center must NOT contain a conventional symbol.

Do not place:

* a star
* an eagle
* a letter
* a number
* a globe
* a skull
* a sword
* a national emblem

The central void is intentionally empty.

The four armored structures visually converge around this void.

The center should be almost completely black, creating a strong contrast against the surrounding metallic surfaces.

---

## HIDDEN INTERNAL ARCHITECTURE

Beneath the four primary metallic plates, place a second layer of dark translucent material.

This internal layer is visible only through the narrow gaps between the armor structures.

It produces a **deep emerald-green internal glow/reflection**.

The green must appear to originate from **inside the badge**, not from paint or surface decoration.

It should look like:

**a classified internal authentication system embedded beneath the armor.**

The emerald should be:

* dark
* muted
* translucent
* recessed
* sophisticated
* physically integrated
* non-neon

The green must never dominate the silver metal.

The metallic armor remains the primary visual element.

---

## HIDDEN FIFTH STRUCTURE

The badge represents four publicly visible SDD domains while secretly containing a fifth structure.

Do NOT display an obvious fifth star.

Do NOT create five equal sections.

Do NOT place a literal five-point star anywhere.

Instead, integrate the fifth structure **inside the central architecture**.

In its normal state, the viewer should only perceive:

**four major armored structures surrounding a central void.**

The fifth structure should remain visually ambiguous and concealed within the layered geometry.

It should appear to be physically present beneath the four visible structures.

The secret must feel **engineered into the badge rather than added afterward**.

---

## PARALLAX CONSTRUCTION

The badge uses layered geometry to create a strong **parallax effect**.

The four major armor assemblies must sit at slightly different depths.

When viewed directly:

The badge reads as one unified geometric diamond.

When viewed from a slight angle:

* the layers separate visually
* recessed channels become visible
* the emerald internal material becomes more apparent
* additional central geometry begins to emerge
* the badge appears deeper and more mechanically complex

The parallax effect must be produced by **actual layered construction**, not by a printed optical illusion.

---

## MATERIAL

The primary material is **precision-machined dark silver and gunmetal**.

The metal should resemble high-grade military/aerospace hardware.

Use:

* dark brushed steel
* gunmetal
* graphite metal
* blackened steel
* polished silver edge highlights

The surfaces must contain subtle realistic machining imperfections.

Include:

* extremely fine scratches
* directional brushing
* microscopic surface texture
* small manufacturing marks
* controlled wear

Do not make the metal mirror-polished.

Do not make it chrome.

Do not make it glossy like jewelry.

It should look like **elite military hardware manufactured to extremely high tolerances**.

---

## EDGE TREATMENT

All major plates have extremely sharp, clean machined edges.

The outer edges should catch controlled highlights.

The bevels should be clearly visible.

The contrast should be:

**dark metal body → bright machined edge → black recessed gap → emerald internal layer.**

The edges should make the badge look physically manufactured from multiple pieces of metal.

---

## GEOMETRIC LANGUAGE

The entire badge must use a consistent language of:

* straight lines
* sharp angles
* triangular planes
* narrow channels
* controlled symmetry
* architectural geometry
* aerospace engineering
* military hardware

Avoid curves except where absolutely necessary for internal mechanical transitions.

The design should feel like a fusion of:

**advanced military armor + aerospace engineering + classified technology + sovereign military authority.**

## OPTIONAL AUTHENTICATION STATE

If generating the **authenticated/classified version**, preserve exactly the same physical badge.

Do not redesign it.

Instead, show the internal system becoming active.

The emerald channels become slightly brighter.

The hidden fifth geometry becomes more clearly visible inside the central architecture.

A very subtle deep crimson trace may appear momentarily as the authentication transition.

The final activated state should resolve into:

**dark gunmetal + silver + muted emerald.**

The crimson is an authentication response, not a permanent badge color.

## PROPORTIONS

Maintain strict bilateral symmetry.

The badge should have:

* a sharp top point
* a sharp bottom point
* symmetrical left/right points
* balanced upper and lower masses
* a relatively compact central void
* large metallic quadrant plates
* narrow internal separation channels

The four primary plates should dominate approximately **90% of the visible badge**.

The recessed emerald architecture should remain secondary.

The central void should be small but visually powerful.

## MOUNTING

Show the badge mounted on **black military tactical fabric**.

The fabric should be heavy, tightly woven and realistic.

Use subtle stitching around the badge mounting area.

The badge should appear securely attached to a modern military uniform or tactical plate carrier.

Do not show the entire person.

Keep the focus entirely on the insignia.

---

## CAMERA

Extreme close-up product photography.

Camera approximately perpendicular to the badge.

The badge occupies most of the image.

Slight perspective is acceptable, but maintain the badge's symmetrical geometry.

Use shallow depth of field only on the surrounding fabric.

The badge itself must remain extremely sharp.

---

## LIGHTING

Use controlled cinematic studio lighting.

Primary light:

soft directional overhead/side light revealing the metal bevels.

Secondary light:

very subtle fill light.

Internal emerald material:

subtle self-illumination from beneath the metal.

The emerald should illuminate the recesses but should NOT cast a large green glow onto the surrounding fabric.

Use realistic reflections across the machined metal.

The image should be dark overall while retaining excellent detail.

---

Prioritize:

**photorealistic materials, physically believable construction, realistic shadows, realistic reflections, precision machining and convincing scale.**
