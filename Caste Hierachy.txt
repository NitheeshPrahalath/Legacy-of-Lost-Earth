## THE BIOLOGICAL CASTE SYSTEM (2120)

### Class 4: The Foundation (The General Citizenry)

* **Demographic Size:** 95% of the population.
* **Living Conditions:** Comfortable, heavily standardized, and entirely synthetic. Everyone is guaranteed a climate-controlled micro-apartment, a state-assigned career, and mandatory technical education. Violent crime is virtually non-existent due to omni-present surveillance (AI predictive policing).
* **Diet:** 100% Synthetic. They consume nutrient-dense printed meals, algae-based protein slabs, and carbohydrate gels designed to simulate flavor and maximize worker efficiency.
* **Psychological Profile:** Highly docile. Because they do not experience starvation or homelessness, the majority believe the system is benevolent. They are deliberately kept unaware of what they are missing. They trade absolute freedom for absolute security.

### Class 3: The Enterprise Sector (Corporate Liaisons & Business Owners)

* **Demographic Size:** 4.9% of the population.
* **Living Conditions:** Highly luxurious but biologically restricted. These are the industrialists, tech-magnates, and logistical lords who manage the global infrastructure in partnership with the state. They live in sprawling penthouse suites above the smog layer, own advanced civilian technology (like high-end Torrent-series Aero-Striders), and hold immense financial wealth.
* **Diet:** 100% Synthetic, but premium tier. They eat artificially constructed meals that look and texturally feel exactly like real food (e.g., vat-grown Wagyu beef, hyper-realistic printed fruits), but it is still entirely lab-created.
* **Psychological Profile:** Ambitious and fiercely competitive. They possess everything except true political power and authentic nature. Their ultimate goal is to buy or marry their way into Class 2.

### Class 2: The Directorate (Government Officials & Bureaucracy)

* **Demographic Size:** 0.9% of the population.
* **Living Conditions:** These individuals hold actual state power—judges, regional governors, military generals, and intelligence directors. They live in secured, fortified governmental sectors equipped with military-grade defense systems.
* **Diet (The Verdant Divide):** This class is fractured into two distinct biological tiers:
* *Lower-Level Officials:* Still restricted to premium synthetic food.
* *High-Level Officials:* Allowed access to **The Biosphere Rations**. They are permitted to consume real, soil-grown vegetables, genuine dairy, and real fruits. In this society, handing a guest a real, bruised, imperfect apple is the ultimate flex of absolute authority.


* **Psychological Profile:** Paranoid and ruthless. The internal politics are lethal, as the gap between eating lab-paste and eating real fruit is the most significant status barrier in human history.

### Class 1: The Sovereign Elite (The Apex)

* **Demographic Size:** 0.01% of the population.
* **Living Conditions:** The architects of the world. Global chancellors, hereditary megacorp owners, and the absolute highest military commanders. They do not live in the dense, towering arcologies with the rest of humanity. They live in secluded, heavily guarded "Eden Enclaves"—massive, climate-controlled geodesic domes where real weather is simulated, and forests, rivers, and wildlife are preserved.
* **Diet:** 100% Organic. They dine on extinct heritage crops, wild-caught fish from private synthetic oceans, and orchard fruits.
* **Psychological Profile:** They view themselves as the custodians of the human race. They justify their hoarding of nature by believing the lower classes would simply destroy the earth again if given the chance.
