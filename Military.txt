## I. THE FIVE BRANCHES OF NATIONAL DEFENSE

The branches retain their structural designations but operate with strict nationalistic objectives, defending borders and projecting power against rival superpowers.

1. **Territorial Pacification Vanguard (TPV):**
* *Role:* The National Army and Mechanized Infantry. They are responsible for border defense, ground invasions, and holding contested territory. The TPV utilizes heavy mag-lev armor, autonomous quadruped weapon platforms, and highly trained infantry equipped with exoskeleton combat suits.


2. **Hydro-Resource Armada (HRA):**
* *Role:* The National Navy. Their mandate is to secure vital international shipping lanes, control the oceans, and project power via massive carrier strike groups. They operate desalinization dreadnoughts, stealth submersibles, and mobile offshore command fortresses.


3. **Atmospheric Supremacy Command (ASC):**
* *Role:* The National Air Force. Responsible for strategic bombing, close air support, and maintaining absolute air superiority over national airspace. They operate the **Kestrel-V Apex**, hypersonic interceptors, and high-altitude drone swarms.


4. **Orbital Strike & Logistics (OSL):**
* *Role:* The National Space Force. They control the nation’s satellite grid, maintain GPS/quantum-communications, and defend against ICBMs. They project power through orbital kinetic platforms and secure national mining interests on the Moon (Helium-3 reserves).


5. **The Verdant Guard:**
* *Role:* Elite Special Operations & State Security (Tier 1). Moving away from agricultural policing, they are now the nation's premier covert operations branch. They handle deep-infiltration, sabotage of enemy arcologies, counter-terrorism, and personal protection of the nation's Class 1 leadership.



---

## II. THE COMMISSIONED OFFICER HIERARCHY

The command structure is a modernized evolution of traditional military ranks. To command in 2120 requires advanced neural-processing cybernetics and a mastery of multi-domain warfare.

*(Note: The Enlisted/NCO tiers—comprising professional Troopers, Sergeants, and Warrant-Techs—form the backbone of the force, but strategic command begins at the Commissioned tier below).*

### Junior Command (Company / Squadron Level)

* **01. Sub-Prime (Equivalent: Lieutenant / Ensign):** Fresh graduates from the national military academies. They lead infantry platoons or serve as junior bridge officers on HRA vessels. They rely heavily on AI tactical overlays to manage their units.
* **02. Captain-Prime (Equivalent: Captain / Lieutenant):** Company commanders and ASC flight leaders. They have proven their ability to handle live combat without being overwhelmed by the massive influx of battlefield sensor data.

### Field Command (Battalion / Regiment / Ship Level)

* **03. Major-Tactical (Equivalent: Major / Lt. Commander):** Battalion executive officers and tactical planners. They often operate from mobile command hubs, coordinating drone strikes, artillery, and infantry movements in real-time.
* **04. Commander-Regent (Equivalent: Lt. Colonel / Commander):** Squadron commanders and leaders of armored battalions. In the HRA, they command nimble stealth destroyers or submarine hunter-killers.
* **05. Colonel-Logis (Equivalent: Colonel / Captain):** Regiment commanders and captains of capital ships (Cruisers/Carriers). A Colonel-Logis manages not just combat, but the complex energy grids, hydrogen-cell supply chains, and maintenance schedules of a massive fighting force.

### Strategic Command (Flag Officers / General Staff)

* **06. Brigadier-Matrix (Equivalent: Brigadier General / Commodore - 1 Star):** Commanders of entire brigades or orbital logistics stations. The "Matrix" designation indicates they now have direct access to the nation's strategic AI defense grid.
* **07. Sector-General / Sector-Admiral (Equivalent: Major General / Rear Admiral - 2 Star):** Division commanders or Carrier Strike Group commanders. They are responsible for an entire theater sector (e.g., defending a 500-mile stretch of the national border or a specific ocean quadrant).
* **08. Theater-General / Theater-Admiral (Equivalent: Lt. General / Vice Admiral - 3 Star):** Corps commanders. They command massive geographical zones and coordinate joint operations across the TPV, HRA, and ASC.
* **09. High General / High Admiral (Equivalent: General / Admiral - 4 Star):** The Chiefs of Staff for their respective branches. They operate from the nation's fortified capital, advising the civilian/corporate leadership on global warfare strategy.
* **10. Grand Marshal of the State (Equivalent: Field Marshal / 5-Star):** The absolute highest wartime rank. Appointed only during global conflicts, this individual holds supreme command over all five military branches of the nation, outranking everyone except the Head of State.

The previously selected Parallax Shield becomes the official SDD master badge.

Not a medieval shield.

Not a heraldic crest.

The four primary structural elements converge toward the center.

From the front, they create the visual impression of **four sovereign forces locking together around one central authority.**

### Public interpretation

Four military domains.

### Actual reality

There is a fifth structure hidden inside the badge.

# IV. THE PARALLAX CONSTRUCTION

The center of the SDD badge contains a precision-milled recessed cavity.

Inside that cavity is the hidden fifth structure.

From ordinary viewing:

**FOUR**

From the correct authorized interaction:

**FIVE**

The fifth structure should not simply be painted green.

It is a separate micro-machined layer embedded beneath the visible shield.

Its material is dark, almost black metallic material with a subtle emerald structural response.

The fifth element is therefore physically present in every SDD master badge.

The ordinary soldier simply cannot properly perceive it.

# V. THE VERDANT SECURITY MECHANISM

The earlier 45-degree-only concept is visually excellent but should **not be sufficient by itself**.

A normal soldier could theoretically see the correct angle accidentally.

Therefore the final canon mechanism combines:

### Parallax + Blood Authentication

The fifth element becomes visible only when the badge receives an authorized biological sample.

A microscopic capillary seam exists in the center of the shield.

To activate the badge, a Verdant Guard operative presses a fingertip against the concealed authentication point.

The badge draws an extremely small blood sample.

The internal system checks the biological signature.

If the sample is unauthorized:

Nothing happens.

The badge remains an ordinary SDD badge.

If the sample belongs to an authorized Verdant Guard operative:

The internal microfluidic channels activate.

The fifth structure becomes visible through the parallax geometry.

A **dark muted-emerald glow** travels through the internal channels.

It should look like a controlled chemical reaction rather than an LED.

No neon green.

No hologram.

No obvious sci-fi glow.

The fifth point appears to be **inside the metal itself.**

The effect lasts only briefly.

Then the badge returns to its normal appearance.

This preserves the original narrative principle that the Verdant Guard is literally hidden inside the institution. The source's final concept already establishes the blood-activated fifth star and muted emerald response.

# 30. CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The entire SDD visual system follows one principle:

> **Four are visible. Five were always present.**

The badge should never announce the secret.

It should make the secret feel like it was **physically built into the organization from the beginning.**

The new reference image is therefore the definitive visual foundation for every SDD insignia going forward:

**four armored diamond structures + central void + recessed emerald architecture + precision-machined gunmetal construction.**

**For future image generation, use the attached badge as the visual master reference.** The branch badges should be treated as *variations of this exact physical construction*, not redesigned from scratch.
