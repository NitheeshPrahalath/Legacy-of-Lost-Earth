### Global context

These 30 countries would have a combined survivor population of roughly **820–840 million people**.

If the world had **10.46 billion** people before the catastrophe:

* **85% die immediately:** ≈ **8.89 billion** deaths.
* **15% survive:** ≈ **1.57 billion** humans.
* About **730–750 million**(7.17%) survivors would then be spread across the rest of the world's countries.

These numbers create a setting where even the largest surviving nations have populations small enough to struggle with defending territory, maintaining infrastructure, and rebuilding civilization while still having enough people to remain major powers.
